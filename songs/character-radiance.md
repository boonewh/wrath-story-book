🗡️ "Only the Light" — Retro-Synthwave (Driving / Neon Synth-Pop)

**CHARACTER ANTHEM for RADIANCE** — the sword of Yaniel, sung in the weapon's own voice. The direct **sequel to "Raise Me Higher"** ([songs/session6-alt.md](session6-alt.md) — the Chapter 6 track Suno auto-titled *"Carry Me"* off the chorus when the title field was left blank, and the name stuck).

The distance between the two is the point. In Ch 6 Radiance was an object *asking* — wake me, name me, raise me, carry me. By Ch 19 it is a participant with a claim on the war itself. **Light is the only language it has** — it cannot warn, refuse, or forgive in words. It can only come up white or go dark, and it has spent three hundred miles of road being read correctly or not.

**The song's argument:** Radiance was at Drezen's gates when Yaniel fell, and the crusade she died for lost. It has already lost this war once. Then fifty years in the dark and a glass case in Kenabres — kept *safe*, which is the insult. So what it wants is not to be raised and not to be carried. **It wants to be spent.**

## Fourth draft — what changed and why

**Draft 1** was too subtle to identify as Radiance. **Draft 2** fixed that and failed in Suno on meter — lines ended awkwardly, nothing balanced. **Draft 3** fixed the meter and was close, but **spent its chorus on the dimming at the cell** — a brief moment, gone, too small to hold a hook and with nowhere to soar.

**Draft 4 rebuilds the chorus around the war.** `README.md` and `lore/worldwound.md` establish that the Mendevian Crusades are one continuous war — *"ongoing so long that crusaders no longer count campaigns… more a permanent military state than a discrete operation."* So a weapon that was at Drezen's gates in the Fourth Crusade and is at them again in the Fifth is in **the same war**, and can say so. That is the lasting claim the dimming could never be:

> *I have already lost this war once, at that gate. / Then fifty years behind glass. Don't make me wait. / I am not here to be carried. I am here to be spent. / Take me back up that road, and I will say what I meant.*

The museum is the antagonist of the chorus. A glass case kept it whole for fifty years, and being kept whole is exactly the wrong thing to do with it. That grows the Ch 6 line *"I am not a trophy anymore"* into something with teeth, and it turns *carry me* into a stage the weapon has moved past rather than a plea it repeats — **the partnership isn't stated, it's demanded.**

**Also corrected: what Radiance actually flared at.** Draft 3 had it burning when the Sword of Valor unfurled. Ch 18 is more precise and much better — the banner drops, and the sword does nothing. It comes up **slow**, and flares only after **the gates go in** and the Fifth Crusade walks through. It did not answer the symbol. It waited for the gate. Verse 4 now runs *"Then the banner came down the tower and I held. I didn't burn. / I was waiting for the gate. And the gate went in. My turn."*

**The dimming is demoted to [Verse 3], where it does better work.** Paired against verse 2 — the false Iomedae, where Caleth read the light *wrong* — the cell becomes the moment he read it **right**, and the two together are the partnership arc that the bridge's *"I said yes"* then completes. The verse ends *"He got it right that time. And I never said a word at all."*

Held from earlier drafts, unchanged in intent: rhymed metered couplets with even line lengths (the fix that made draft 3 generate cleanly); identity planted early and landed late (hammer/sword/glaive and *a blade has no mouth* → *a blade has no way to say* → **Yaniel** and **Radiance** at the close); the Ch 18 parry instead of a lying tell; no *door* (Thane's motif — verse 3 says *cell*); no session count as in-world time; Aravashnial not written as old or written in at all; the *carry me* callback flagged as a former line, now in the final chorus (*"that's an old song. This is new"*).

## Style (paste into Suno style box)

Propulsive 1980s neon synth-pop — fast, urgent, retro-futurist, cinematic. ONE register throughout, no genre change. Bright saw-lead synth hook cycling as the main melody from the first bar to the last. Relentless four-on-the-floor kick, huge gated-reverb snare on 2 and 4, driving eighth-note analog bass pulse, glittering arpeggios underneath. Emotional male vocal pushed high and urgent, phrased legato and unhurried, every line sung through to its last word — tight and close in the verses, belted wide open on the chorus, near-spoken in the outro. Bright major-key surface with real ache under it, NOT an empty party track. Dynamic swells WITHIN the one register — verses driving and contained, chorus blooming enormous, bridge stripped to bass pulse and vocal. No tempo changes, no key changes. No acoustic guitar, no strummed strings, no country, no folk, no metal. Synthesizers only. No instrumental padding between sections. End sharp on one bright synth hit, not a fade.

## Troubleshooting

**If lines still end abruptly:** the meter should now carry it, but if a specific line gets clipped, that line is too long for the bar — cut a syllable rather than re-tagging. The longest lines in the song are in verse 2 and the bridge; those are the first places to look.

**If it doesn't read as a weapon singing:** that was draft 1's failure. The identifiers are load-bearing — *hammer / sword / glaive* and *a blade has no mouth* in V1, *a blade has no way to say* in V2, *Yaniel* and *Radiance* at the close. If Suno mumbles any of those, regenerate rather than accept it.

**If "white" comes back sounding like "right":** V1's *"I come up white, or I go dark"* states both halves in one line precisely so the contrast fixes the vowel. If it still fails, tag it `[Verse 1 - enunciate]` before changing the word.

**If the chorus lift doesn't arrive:** "I am not here to be carried. I am here to be spent" is the pivot and has to open up — it is the biggest claim in the song. Tag it `[Chorus - vocal soars, synths bloom brighter]`.

**If "My turn" at the end of verse 4 gets buried:** it is the payoff of seventy years and should land alone, hard, into the final chorus. `[Verse 4 - final two words full stop, hit them]`.

**If acoustic guitar or any strummed string appears:** the recurring Suno failure on this project. Regenerate with `NO acoustic guitar, NO strummed strings, NO country, NO folk — pure electronic, synthesizers only`.

**If the bridge doesn't strip back:** "I said yes" is the emotional center and needs air. `[Bridge - bass pulse and vocal only, no drums]`.

**If "I have never said yes before" gets swallowed:** slow it — `[Bridge - final line half time]`.

**If the outro is sung big:** it must not be. `[Outro - near spoken, quiet, no drums]`. "He still calls me Radiance" is a statement, not a climax.

## Canon Notes

Everything is on the page — no invented canon:

- **hammer / sword / glaive** — Radiance takes a different form for whoever holds it: a warhammer for Korroc, a longsword for Nageru, a glaive for Caleth, each sized to the wielder's reach (`lore/items.md`).
- **"He never blocks. He slides… half a step of ground"** — Ch 18, verbatim in substance: *"Caleth did not block. He slid, and let the claws come past his ribs by a finger's width, and caught the shadow's arm on Radiance's flat and turned it aside, and the whole cost of the exchange to him was a half-step of ground. Then the blade came off the parry in one unbroken motion."* This is Caleth's **Virtuous Bravo** paladin archetype — panache, opportune parry and riposte, *"he does not charge. He angles, withdraws, re-enters at the seam"* (`characters/caleth.md`). **Replaces draft 2's "the half-step back he takes before he lies,"** which was uncanonical characterization and drifted into Thane's read-the-tells register.
- **"Three hundred miles of road"** — Korroc's measure of the march in Ch 18 (*"over three hundred miles of road"*), used instead of a calendar. **Avoids draft 1's "ten seasons,"** which was session-count leaking in as in-world time.
- **"I have already lost this war once"** — the chorus's central claim, and it is literal. `lore/worldwound.md`: the **Fourth Crusade** was *"a holding action that became a disaster,"* and **the paladin Yaniel died at Drezen's gates** carrying this sword. `README.md` and the same file establish the crusades as one continuous war — *"ongoing so long that crusaders no longer count campaigns… more a permanent military state than a discrete operation."* A weapon present at that gate in the Fourth and at it again in the Fifth is in the same war and entitled to say so.
- **"Then fifty years behind glass"** — the sword was thought lost for half a century until a salvage team recovered it into the **Kenabres museum**, where the Ch 6 song opens (`lore/items.md`). The chorus treats the case as the insult rather than the rescue — which is the grown-up version of Ch 6's *"I am not a trophy anymore,"* held back and reused verbatim-in-spirit in the final chorus (*"No glass. No stand. No trophy. No price."*).
- **Verse 3, the cell** — Ch 17. ⚠ **Note the specifics:** the cell with the *dead* sigils was the antimagic field; Radiance dimmed at **the other one — still lit, glowing red** — and Caleth backed away and gave it the width of the room. Thane: *"he's giving it a berth like it's a snake."* **What was behind it is never explained**, which is why the lyric says only *the light on it was not mine.* The word **door is deliberately avoided** — doors are Thane's motif.
- **"Something wore my lady's face"** — Ch 15, the succubus in Iomedae's shape. Radiance was forged for a paladin of the Inheritor, so *my lady* is Iomedae. Caleth *"watched himself lower Radiance"*; his refrain *"knowing wasn't enough"* is given back in the blade's mouth and turned into shared culpability (*"That makes two"*). **Whether the blade lit in recognition or in devotion is left open on purpose** — the whole thesis is that light is ambiguous and the man has to read it.
- **The bridge** — Interlude 2. Three failed bindings (*"It rejected an incompatible structure"* / Thane: *"It's got a latch already… it's holding the frame shut while you do it"*), then the working seated *"like a weld — pressure, heat, patience, no force,"* the glow going deep and steady *"the way a bank of coals goes when you have finally got the air right,"* and then it **accepted him.** *"A smith read the grain"* is Thane, who cut the physical channels and says *"I* am *a smith"* in that scene. ⚠ **The elf is NOT written as old** — Aravashnial is an elder by rank, not by age (`characters/npcs.md`) — and draft 3 leaves him out of the lyric entirely rather than risk it.
- **"Fifty years in the dark, and a glass case on a stand"** — Yaniel fell at Drezen's gates; the sword was thought lost for half a century until a salvage team recovered it into the Kenabres museum, where Ch 6's song opens.
- **Verse 4, and what it flared at** — Ch 18, read closely. Caleth lets the Sword of Valor fall down the tower and **Radiance does nothing.** The cheer comes up, the demons break, the gates go in a quarter of an hour later, the Fifth Crusade walks through — and only *then* does the sword *"begin to glow brighter… it came up slow — and then it flared, one hard clean pulse,"* and *"it did not go all the way down again afterward."* Korroc: *"Yaniel's sword had come home to Drezen with the banner. It seemed to have an opinion about that."* **It did not answer the symbol. It waited for the gate.** Hence *"I held. I didn't burn. / I was waiting for the gate. And the gate went in. My turn."*
- **"a hand that keeps coming back"** — Korroc returning it to Caleth in Ch 6 (*the blade keeps finding your hand*), and the Ch 15 stun where he drops it, takes it up again, and it *"woke warm in his hand."*

**Not used, deliberately:** what the new working actually *does* (unestablished — needs the GM), what was behind the red cell door (never explained — the lyric says only *the light was not mine*), and Caleth's Spireborn / Riftwarden Orphan material (the blade wouldn't know it, and the song is public).
