🗡️ "Only the Light" — Crusader Synthwave (Heavy / Slow-Burn)

**CHARACTER ANTHEM for RADIANCE** — the sword of Yaniel, sung in the weapon's own voice. This is the direct **sequel to "Raise Me Higher"** ([songs/session6-alt.md](session6-alt.md) — the track Suno auto-titled *"Carry Me"* off the chorus when the title field was left blank, and the name stuck).

Ten sessions separate them, and the distance is the whole point. In Chapter 6 Radiance was an object *asking* — wake me, name me, raise me, carry me. By Chapter 19 it is a participant with judgment: it dimmed at a door in Chapter 17 to warn Caleth away from something, it threw a working back across a forge three times in Interlude 2 before consenting to a fourth, and it flared over the recovered Banner at Drezen and never fully dimmed again. The premise of the lyric is that **light is the only language it has** — it cannot warn, cannot refuse, cannot forgive in words. It can only brighten and go low, and it has spent ten sessions being read correctly or not.

Deliberately the same musical family as "Carry Me" so it lands as the sequel — but slower, heavier, and without the euphoria. Same voice, ten sessions older.

## Style (paste into Suno style box)

Heavy crusader synthwave, slow-burning and weighted. ONE register throughout, no genre change. Mid-tempo, patient, relentless. Low analog synth bass with real mass under everything, a slow arpeggiated figure cycling steady rather than sparkling, wide melancholy pads, gated-reverb drums hitting late and hard. Solo male vocal, weathered and close-mic'd — restrained and almost flat in the verses, opening and cracking wide on the chorus, near-spoken in the outro. Cinematic and holy, but tired and certain: something very old that has decided something. Dynamic swells WITHIN the one register — verses low and held, chorus blooming enormous, bridge stripped to bass pulse and voice before the last lift. The final chorus is the only full brightness in the song; nothing before it is euphoric or triumphant. No tempo changes, no key changes. No acoustic guitar, no strummed strings, no country, no folk, no metal. Synthesizers only. End on one sustained chord decaying to silence.

## Troubleshooting

**If it comes out euphoric / sounds like "Carry Me":** that is the failure mode — the sequel must not be the original. Push `heavy, slow-burning, restrained, NOT euphoric, NOT a party track, weight over brightness`. The lift belongs to the Final Chorus alone.

**If acoustic guitar or any strummed string appears:** the recurring Suno failure on this project. Regenerate with `NO acoustic guitar, NO strummed strings, NO country, NO folk — pure electronic, synthesizers only`.

**If the bridge doesn't strip back:** the "I said yes" line needs air around it — it is the emotional center of the whole track. Tag it `[Bridge - bass pulse and vocal only, no drums]`.

**If "I have never said yes before" gets swallowed:** slow it. That line is the thesis. Try `[Bridge - final line half time]`.

**If the outro is sung big:** it must not be. `[Outro - near spoken, quiet, no drums]`. "He keeps coming back" is a statement, not a climax.

## Canon Notes

Everything in the lyric is on the page — no invented canon:

- **"I dimmed at the door"** — Ch 17: Radiance dims as Caleth approaches the red-glowing cell door; he backs away and gives it the width of the room. Thane: *"he's giving it a berth like it's a snake."*
- **"a woman wearing a face I know" / "I watched him go"** — Ch 15: the succubus wearing Iomedae's face; Caleth *"watched himself lower Radiance."* His refrain *"knowing wasn't enough"* is quoted back in the blade's mouth.
- **"Three times I threw it back"** — Interlude 2 (Ch 18.5). Korroc: *"Temperamental thing."* Caleth: *"It rejected an incompatible structure."* Thane: *"It's got a latch already."*
- **"laid it in like a weld"** — Aravashnial seating the working: *pressure, heat, patience, no force*; the glow *"the way a bank of coals goes when you have finally got the air right"*; then it **accepted him.**
- **"She fell at those gates"** — Yaniel fell at Drezen carrying it. Ch 18: the Banner flies, Radiance flares one hard pulse and *"did not go all the way down again."* Korroc: *"It seemed to have an opinion about that."*
- **"a hand that keeps coming back"** — Korroc returning it to Caleth in Ch 6 (*the blade keeps finding your hand*), and the Ch 15 stun where he drops it and takes it up again and it *"woke warm in his hand."*

**Not used, deliberately:** what the new working actually *does* (unestablished, needs the GM), and Caleth's Spireborn / Riftwarden Orphan material (the blade would not know it, and the song is public).
