🗡️ "Only the Light" — Retro-Synthwave (Driving / "Blinding Lights" energy)

**CHARACTER ANTHEM for RADIANCE** — the sword of Yaniel, sung in the weapon's own voice. The direct **sequel to "Raise Me Higher"** ([songs/session6-alt.md](session6-alt.md) — the Chapter 6 track Suno auto-titled *"Carry Me"* off the chorus when the title field was left blank, and the name stuck).

The distance between the two is the point. In Ch 6 Radiance was an object *asking* — wake me, name me, raise me, carry me. By Ch 19 it is a participant with judgment: it dimmed at a warded cell door to warn Caleth off, it threw a working back across a forge three times before consenting to a fourth, and it flared over the recovered Banner at Drezen and never fully dimmed again. **Light is the only language it has** — it cannot warn, refuse, or forgive in words. It can only come up white or go dark, and it has spent three hundred miles of road being read correctly or not.

**Second draft.** The first was too subtle to identify without being told. This one plants the weapon in the opening lines (hammer / sword / glaive — the shapes it takes for different hands), makes the false Iomedae and the prison door legible on one listen, flags the *"carry me"* callback out loud, and lands **Yaniel** and **Radiance** by name at the end.

## Style (paste into Suno style box)

Propulsive 1980s retro-synthwave in the vein of The Weeknd's "Blinding Lights" — fast, urgent, neon, relentlessly driving. ONE register throughout, no genre change. Bright saw-lead synth hook cycling as the main melody the whole way, punchy four-on-the-floor kick, huge gated-reverb snare on 2 and 4, warm pulsing analog bass, glittering arpeggios underneath. Emotional male vocal pushed high and urgent — tight and close in the verses, belting wide open on the chorus, near-spoken in the outro. Retro-futurist and cinematic, with real ache under the brightness: hope and cost braided together, NOT an empty party track. Dynamic swells WITHIN the one register — verses driving and contained, chorus blooming enormous, bridge stripped to bass pulse and vocal before the last lift. No tempo changes, no key changes. No acoustic guitar, no strummed strings, no country, no folk, no metal. Synthesizers only. No instrumental padding between sections. End sharp on one bright synth hit, not a fade.

## Troubleshooting

**If it doesn't read as a weapon singing:** that was the first draft's failure. The identifiers are load-bearing — *hammer / sword / glaive* in V1, *a blade has no way to say* in V2, *Yaniel* and *Radiance* at the close. If Suno mumbles any of those, regenerate rather than accept it.

**If the chorus lift doesn't arrive:** "I used to sing carry me" is the pivot and has to open up. Tag it `[Chorus - vocal soars, synths bloom brighter]`.

**If acoustic guitar or any strummed string appears:** the recurring Suno failure on this project. Regenerate with `NO acoustic guitar, NO strummed strings, NO country, NO folk — pure electronic, synthesizers only`.

**If the bridge doesn't strip back:** "I said yes" is the emotional center and needs air. `[Bridge - bass pulse and vocal only, no drums]`.

**If "I have never said yes before" gets swallowed:** slow it — `[Bridge - final line half time]`.

**If the outro is sung big:** it must not be. `[Outro - near spoken, quiet, no drums]`. "And he still calls me Radiance" is a statement, not a climax.

## Canon Notes

Everything is on the page — no invented canon:

- **hammer / sword / glaive** — Radiance takes a different form for whoever holds it: a warhammer for Korroc, a longsword for Nageru, a glaive for Caleth, each sized to the wielder's reach (`lore/items.md`).
- **"Three hundred miles of road"** — Korroc's measure of the march in Ch 18 (*"over three hundred miles of road"*), used here instead of a calendar. **Avoids the first draft's "ten seasons," which was session-count leaking in as in-world time.**
- **"I dimmed at the door"** — Ch 17. ⚠ **Note the specifics:** the cell with the *dead* sigils was the antimagic field; Radiance dimmed at **the other one — still lit, glowing red** — and Caleth backed away and gave it the width of the room. Thane: *"he's giving it a berth like it's a snake."* **What was behind that red door is never explained**, which is why the lyric says only *the light on it was not mine.*
- **"Something wore my lady's face"** — Ch 15, the succubus in Iomedae's shape. Radiance was forged for a paladin of the Inheritor, so *my lady* is Iomedae. Caleth *"watched himself lower Radiance"*; his refrain *"knowing wasn't enough"* is given back in the blade's mouth.
- **The bridge** — Interlude 2. Three failed bindings (*"It rejected an incompatible structure"* / Thane: *"It's got a latch already"*), then Aravashnial seating it *"like a weld — pressure, heat, patience, no force,"* the glow going deep and steady *"the way a bank of coals goes when you have finally got the air right,"* and then it **accepted him.** ⚠ **The elf is NOT written as old** — Aravashnial is an elder by rank, not by age (`characters/npcs.md`: *"do NOT write him as an 'old man'"*).
- **"Half a century in the dark, and a museum after that"** — Yaniel fell at Drezen's gates; the sword was thought lost for fifty years until a salvage team recovered it into the Kenabres museum, where Ch 6's song opens.
- **The final chorus** — Ch 18: the Banner flies over Drezen and Radiance flares one hard pulse and *"did not go all the way down again."* Korroc: *"It seemed to have an opinion about that."*
- **"a hand that keeps coming back"** — Korroc returning it to Caleth in Ch 6 (*the blade keeps finding your hand*), and the Ch 15 stun where he drops it, takes it up again, and it *"woke warm in his hand."*

**Not used, deliberately:** what the new working actually *does* (unestablished — needs the GM), and Caleth's Spireborn / Riftwarden Orphan material (the blade wouldn't know it, and the song is public).
