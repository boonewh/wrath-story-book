# The Mountain Does Not Kneel

## Suno Prompt

Epic dwarven power metal, crushing low-tuned electric guitars, thunderous double-kick drums, pounding war percussion, deep resonant male baritone lead vocals, massive layered dwarven male choir, rhythmic hammer-on-anvil percussion, triumphant folk-metal melodies, powerful marching rhythms, gritty commanding vocals, huge gang-shouted choruses, cinematic mountain-hall atmosphere, heroic guitar harmonies, explosive melodic guitar solo, 138 BPM. Heavy, defiant, anthemic, powerful, with a huge unforgettable singalong chorus. No pop vocals, no electronic beats.
