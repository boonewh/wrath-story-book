Caleth's song for [chapters/22 — the scrying, the cage, the name.md]. ## Style (paste into Suno style box)

Cold, precise dark synth-ambient, 100 BPM. Sparse minimal beat — a single clipped percussive tick, like a counted second, underlies most of the track. Icy analog synth pulses, restrained and controlled, never swelling into warmth. Male vocal, flat and measured, almost reciting rather than singing — the emotion is in what's withheld, not what's shown. No power chorus, no anthemic lift, no war-choir. The Bridge is the one place control cracks: vocal strains, distortion bleeds in, a single dissonant swell — then it pulls back immediately, colder than before. Mood: a ledger kept in the dark, a man doing arithmetic instead of grieving. NOT warm, NOT orchestral, NOT heroic — clinical, tense, quietly devastating. Ends abruptly on the last spoken name, no fade.

## Notes

This stays grounded in the actual events of the session rather than a metaphor — kettle, dossier, dragon fight, the search, the second scrying, the torture — while keeping the restrained, controlled delivery that's become Caleth's distinct register among the party's chapter songs, the opposite pole from the crusader anthem and the chapel ballad.
