⚒️ "Open It Again" — Slow Doom-Metal Dirge

The **Session 16** song for [chapters/16-two-brothers-of-the-same-forge.md](../chapters/16-two-brothers-of-the-same-forge.md) — Korroc's first per-session song, and a deliberate **counterweight** to his character anthem [songs/character-korroc.md](character-korroc.md) ("The Anvil Does Not Move"). That song is the immovable one: drums forward, stomping, certain. This one is the same dwarf on the day his certainty cost somebody else blood, and it moves at a funeral's pace because he cannot get out from under it.

The through-line a first-time listener needs, with no knowledge of the story: **a man whose whole craft is opening his hand gives a weapon back to something that looked clean, and it turns on his friend; he closes his hand once, in rage, on a face he has hated for seventy-five years; and then, the same hour, he opens it again for a stranger who does not deserve it either.** Verse 1 is the read he trusted and the reason he trusted it — he cannot leave the wrong tool in a working man's hand. The chorus is the bite: no bargain, no arrangement, just a hungry thing and whoever stood nearest with his palm out. Verse 2 is the well running dry — he poured healing on everyone all morning and had nothing left when he finally looked at the one man he could not forgive. The bridge is the disagreement with his cousin, which he loses and would lose again. The final chorus is the turn the whole song exists for: **any fool can break iron that's already ruined; the craft is what you do with metal somebody else beat wrong.**

**No names, no plot.** The swordsman, the succubus, the traitor and the kneeling brother are all in there as shapes only — the song stays inside Korroc's chest. Paste the LYRICS block into Suno's lyrics box, the STYLE block into the style box, and "Open It Again" into the title field.

## Style (paste into Suno style box)

Character count: 880 (spaces included — under the ~1,000 style-box cap, the one real Suno limit)

Slow doom-metal dirge, 62 BPM, funeral pace, never speeding up. Enormously heavy downtuned guitars playing long sustained chords, thick fuzzed bass, drums sparse and huge — half-time, tom-heavy, each hit allowed to decay. A real anvil struck on the downbeat, far back in the mix. Male lead vocal only: deep gravel baritone, throaty and worn, low in the chest, plain and unhurried, more stated than sung, words fully intelligible. No clean high singing, no screaming, no backing choir, no church organ, no orchestra. Mood: guilt carried by a working man, grief with the anger burned out of it, exhausted mercy. Weight, not speed. The bridge drops to a single low drone under near-spoken vocal, then the final section returns at the same tempo, heavier, resigned rather than triumphant. Single register start to finish, one key, one tempo. NOT epic, NOT soaring, NOT a victory song.

## Troubleshooting

1. **If it speeds up or turns into a gallop:** This is the most likely failure — Suno hears "metal" and wants momentum. Lead the style box with "62 BPM, funeral pace, half-time drums, no double-kick, never accelerates."
2. **If the chorus comes back triumphant:** Wrong emotion entirely. The chorus is a man admitting he caused something. Add "chorus is heavier but not uplifting, no major-key lift, no anthem."
3. **If a choir or cathedral pad appears:** Same fix as the character anthem — "solo male vocal only, no choir, no church organ." Korroc's faith is a forge, not a cathedral.
4. **If the vocal comes back clean or melodic:** "Gruff solo male baritone, gravel grit, spoken-edge delivery, low register" up front. Do not push to a death-growl — the words have to land.
5. **If the spoken bridge gets sung:** Add (spoken) inline after each bridge line. The bridge is the disagreement with Thane and it has to sound like a man stating a decision he already knows he'll carry.
6. **Keep it distinct from "The Anvil Does Not Move."** That one is drums-forward, stomping, 100 BPM, certain. This one is slow, guitar-weight-forward, and the whole point is that the anvil moved.
