🔨 "Give Him Back" — Driving Hard Rock

The **Session 18** song for [chapters/18-the-mark-it-chose.md](../chapters/18-the-mark-it-chose.md). Korroc's second per-session song and the loud one — where [songs/session16.md](session16.md) ("Open It Again") is a slow guilty dirge, this is uptempo riff rock with a gang-shout bridge and a guitar solo.

**The whole song is the possession.** A demon pours itself into a man's cousin and comes at him with the cousin's own knives, and he turns them aside without once swinging back, because he has known that hand for fifty-one years and knows exactly where it goes. Then he breaks a stone on another man's word, with no proof of his own, because that is where the soul is supposed to be. No mechanics, no lore, no armor: a listener who has never heard of this campaign gets it on the first chorus — *somebody took my family and I want him back, and I will not hit him to get him.*

The ending is the quiet one: Thane picks up his mother's blade and wipes a blade that has no blood on it, because that is the family rule, and that is how Korroc knows he's home.

> **Alternate kept on file:** [session18-alt-it-chose-me.md](session18-alt-it-chose-me.md) — "It Chose Me," the same musical direction built on the Armor of the Pious taking Torag's mark. Good song, wrong subject: at the table the armor was a magic-item footnote, so a song about it plays as a riddle. Kept in case that beat ever wants its own track.

Driving hard rock, 138 BPM, big open riff, straight rock drums with a heavy backbeat. Classic riff-rock guitars — crunchy, mid-gain, rhythm guitar leading the song, a real ripping guitar solo in the middle. Warm loud bass locked to the kick. Male lead vocal: gravel baritone, rough and belted, shouted on the chorus, fully intelligible. Gang-shout backing vocals on the bridge only, several rough male voices chanting together. Anvil and hammer hits as accents on the downbeat. Mood: protective fury, a man demanding his family back from something that took him, defiant and loud, refusing to strike. Energetic and forward-driving throughout. Outro pulls back to the riff alone under the last lines, quieter but not slower. No choir, no organ, no orchestra, no ballad section, no slow intro, no acoustic, no tempo change, no key change. Single register, one groove.

## Troubleshooting

1. **If it slows down or turns into a ballad:** Lead the style box with "138 BPM driving hard rock, no ballad, no slow intro, never slows." The outro pulls back in volume only — the tempo holds.
2. **If the bridge chant comes back as a solo vocal:** It needs several rough voices. Add "gang-shout chorus of rough male voices in unison on the bridge, shouted not sung."
3. **If the guitar solo is skipped:** Make the tag insistent — `[Guitar solo — long, lead guitar out front, band keeps the riff under it]` — and add "extended guitar solo" to the style box.
4. **If a choir or church organ creeps in:** Same rule as every Korroc song: "solo male vocal only, no choir, no church organ."
5. **If the vocal comes back clean or polished:** "Gravel baritone, rough, belted, shouted on the chorus" up front. Don't push to a growl — "give him back" has to be audible or the song has no subject.
6. **If the outro gets belted like another chorus:** It's the release, not a climax. Tag it "outro quieter, riff only under the vocal, close to spoken on the last two lines."
7. **Keep it distinct from "Open It Again" (session 16).** That one is a 62 BPM doom dirge, guilty and heavy. This one is fast, loud and protective.
