# Get Out of My Skin

The official **Week 18** song for [The Mark It Chose](../chapters/18-the-mark-it-chose.md) — Thane. Driving melodic alternative metal.

This is the demand inside his head during the possession, never spoken to the party. The song stays inside that feeling; it does not narrate the rescue or resolve his silence afterward.
