# Yes, Before I Knew

## Style

Raw mid-90s Seattle grunge, tracked live in a garage, 1994, Drop-D detuned electric guitars, thick distorted power chords against clean fragile arpeggios, Loud-quiet-loud dynamics, Pounding live drums, slightly loose, room mics bleeding, Heavy melodic bass, Male lead vocal slides from cracked near-whisper to a full-throat anguished scream — wounded, intimate, never polished, Gang backing vocals enter only at the final chorus, ragged and unison, Analog warmth, tape hiss, amp hum, minimal production, no gloss, no reverb sheen, Emotionally exposed, slow-burning, sludgy in the heavy parts, brittle and bare in the quiet parts, Single grunge register throughout — no genre shifts, no clean radio polish, Feedback tails, dynamic restraint breaking into catharsis
