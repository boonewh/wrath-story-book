## Troubleshooting

1. **If it sounds like generic metal with no Mongolian character (the whole point):** The traditional instruments and throat singing ARE the request — they must be clearly audible, not decoration. Push `traditional Mongolian instruments prominent and up front — morin khuur horsehead fiddle, tovshuur lute, limbe flute — fused with the guitars, in the style of The HU (hunnu rock); folk instruments clearly audible`. If it still reads as plain metal, name The HU again and foreground the fiddle and lute.

2. **If the throat singing goes UNINTELLIGIBLE:** The English words must still land — the rewrite's whole value was legibility. Lead with `deep kargyraa Mongolian throat singing but fully intelligible diction, every word clearly heard`. If the low khoomei keeps eating the words, drop to `a deep, throated, chant-style male vocal with kargyraa color` so the story survives.

3. **If Suno can't produce throat singing / gives a normal rock vocal:** Tag the vocal explicitly — `Mongolian khoomei throat singing, kargyraa overtone singing, male` — and accept a deep guttural chant-style delivery as the fallback. The gang-chant choruses will carry the Mongolian feel even if the lead won't throat-sing.

4. **If the "NO" gets blended into the line / buried (the user's recurring complaint):** Same fix as the base song — it must be the single biggest, most isolated hit. The `[Breakdown]` sections drop the band out; the word sits alone in ALL CAPS. If Suno still buries it, strengthen to `[Breakdown — ALL instruments STOP, one unison throated war-shout "NO!" alone in silence, then chant NO! NO!]` and add `the word NO is a full-stop hit with dead air around it, unison throated shout at the very front of the mix`.

5. **If it goes too fast / blast-beat:** The HU is a mid-tempo, thunderous, galloping STOMP — not speed metal. Hold it at `epic mid-tempo war-march around 120 BPM, thunderous and driving, NOT blast-beat, NOT fast death metal`. The power is in the weight, not the speed.

6. **If it drifts satanic / chaotic:** This is ancient, ceremonial, tribal war-metal — proud and epic, not demonic. Add `ancient tribal steppe war-metal, ceremonial and proud, NOT satanic, NOT chaotic`.

7. **Single register:** A short morin khuur or flute figure to open is fine and characteristic — but no meditative genre-swap, no soft passage detonating into metal. Stay in the one hunnu-rock register start to finish.
