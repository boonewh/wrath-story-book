# Kin of the Fallen

## Suno Prompt
Melodic hard rock, driving 118 bpm pulse; crunching drop-tuned riffs with tight palm-muted verses, pounding drums, stacked chorus guitar doubles, and an emotional melodic lead solo; dark brooding atmosphere opening into huge anthemic choruses with layered harmonies; powerful male tenor with a raw edge, restrained verses building to a soaring chorus
