# Session 15 — Blinded by a False Light

**Style:** Darksynth / dark-bass synthwave, aggressive and nocturnal, Hard four-on-the-floor kick, snappy gated snare, fast 16th-note arpeggios, heavy overdriven bass synth pulsing like a heartbeat, Menacing minor key, industrial/EBM edge, gritty analog leads, sidechained pumping pads, Urgent, breathless male vocal — driving and desperate in the verses, a sharp cutting hook doubled and half-shouted on the chorus — plate reverb, tape saturation, Relentless nightdrive momentum, tension coiled tight; a hard breakdown drops to throbbing bass and one lone arpeggio, then slams back bigger, Cold neon, chrome and menace, Analog synths only, no acoustic instruments

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(hard arpeggio, pulse slamming in)

[Verse 1]
Midnight. A chapel gone to rot.
Deceiver with a saint's face, but its not.
I read it cold — I named the lie —
and my feet still move. My feet still move.

[Pre-Chorus]
Alarms in every nerve, screaming no, screaming no —
but it burns like the sun, and I can't let go.

[Chorus]
It looked like grace, my God it looked like grace —
a hungry thing in a holy face.
One kiss in the glow and it drinks me dry.
I knew. But I don't know why.
Blinded by a false light.

[Verse 2]
It wears the thing I've loved my whole life,
it wears the beautiful, wears it bright.
Steals my wanting, sells it back —
and I hand it my pulse. I hand it my pulse.

[Pre-Chorus]
Every wire says no, says no, says no —
but I lean to the glow. God, I lean to the glow.

[Chorus]
It looked like grace, my God it looked like grace —
a hungry thing in a holy face.
One kiss in the glow and it drinks me dry.
I knew. But I don't know why.
Blinded by a false light.

[Bridge - hard breakdown, throbbing bass, one arpeggio]
Came back cold. Came back low.
Wiped the gold off my mouth in the afterglow.
It wanted me scared of the true for good —
teach me to flinch from the bright, from the light.
No. No.
I know the false from the holy now.
Eyes open. Eyes open. When the glow comes down.

[Final Chorus - slams back, bigger]
It looked like grace — but I see the teeth.
The softer the shine, the worse underneath.
Come glow at me, come drink me dry —
I know. I know. And I don't break.
I walk the night with open eyes.
No false light. No false light. Takes me twice.

[Outro]
(pulse rides out, arpeggio decays)
It looked like grace…
I know the difference now.
