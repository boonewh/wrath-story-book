# Session 25

**Title:** More Than What We Were

**POV:** Nageru

Why this fits Nageru
What I particularly like about this direction is that it explores Nageru's philosophy rather than his combat abilities.

In this session, he fights demons without hesitation, yet when Arueshalae reaches out through his dream, he is confronted with something more complicated. She's a demon who doesn't want to be one anymore. His faith and judgment are being tested in a way that no physical battle could accomplish.

The line "Sometimes mercy takes more strength than standing face to face with fear" is the heart of the song. It reflects the kind of paladin Nageru has the opportunity to be.

There's also a subtle double meaning. The song can apply to Arueshalae seeking redemption, but it can just as easily apply to anyone who has made mistakes and is struggling to change.
For Nageru, I'm picturing a voice that sounds like it's coming from deep within the earth—controlled, powerful, and almost otherworldly. Not death-metal screaming, but that unmistakable low, vibrating Mongolian throat singing, with the full weight of a heavy metal band behind it.

That should give him a sound that is both spiritual and intimidating, which suits his character particularly well.

## Suno Prompt

Aggressive Mongolian throat-singing heavy metal, extremely deep guttural kargyraa throat vocals as the dominant lead voice throughout the entire song, powerful gravelly bass growls with overtone resonance, traditional Mongolian khoomei throat singing, crushing downtuned distorted electric guitar riffs, thunderous double-kick drums, pounding tribal war percussion, dark droning morin khuur horsehead fiddle, relentless galloping metal rhythms, deep masculine unison throat-chant choruses, raw primal warrior energy, ominous spiritual atmosphere, heavy rhythmic vocal delivery, explosive metal breakdowns, massive low-end production, 110 BPM. No clean tenor singing, no pop vocals, no soft rock, no polished melodic rock choruses.
