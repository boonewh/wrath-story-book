## Suno Style

Dark cinematic 1980s synthwave, synth-rock, male lead vocal, emotional and haunted but determined. Pulsing analog bass, arpeggiated synthesizers, gated reverb drums, atmospheric pads, distant electric guitar, dramatic toms, subtle heartbeat pulse, neon-noir atmosphere. Verses restrained and mysterious with gradually building tension. Pre-choruses rise emotionally. Huge melodic anthemic chorus with layered backing vocals repeating “No shadow.” Instrumental break with soaring 80s electric guitar over driving synth arpeggios. Dream sequence becomes sparse, eerie, and cinematic with low synth drones and heartbeat percussion, then builds explosively into the final chorus. Final chorus should feel desperate, defiant, and personal rather than victorious. Outro strips back to cold synth ambience and ends ominously on the whispered words “No shadow.” Minor key, approximately 105–115 BPM. Dark fantasy filtered through retro 1980s synthwave.
