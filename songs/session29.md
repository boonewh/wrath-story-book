# Session 29 — Burn the Swarm

**Character:** Caleth  
**Point of View:** Caleth  
**Song Title:** Burn the Swarm

This is the confrontation we've been building toward: Caleth finally faces Xanthir Vang.
I want to return to Caleth's harder, more aggressive darksynth style from Blinded by a False Light. This song should have a relentless driving bass, sharp synthesizers, and a chorus that feels almost explosive.
But there's a personal element beneath the battle. Vang has taken Aravashnial, stolen his spellbook, and invaded Caleth's dreams through the warnings of his mysterious mark. When Caleth finally confronts him, the Spiral begins burning again.
The song should feel like controlled anger finally breaking loose. Caleth begins the battle calculating every move, but when Vang's thousands of worms start crawling over him, his control becomes a fight for survival.

## Suno Style

Aggressive 1980s darksynth / dark-bass synthwave, 122 BPM, menacing minor key, industrial EBM edge. Heavy overdriven analog bass, fast 16th-note arpeggios, hard four-on-the-floor kick, snappy gated snare, gritty distorted synth leads, sidechained atmospheric pads. Urgent male tenor vocals, cold and calculated in verses, increasingly angry and breathless. Explosive anthemic chorus with sharp, half-shouted hooks and low layered male backing vocals. Relentless nightdrive momentum, cinematic boss-battle intensity. Fast instrumental break with dueling synth leads. Hard breakdown drops to throbbing bass and one eerie arpeggio, intimate threatening vocals, then accelerates into a massive final chorus with pounding electronic drums and distorted synth walls. Ominous outro with fading bass and whispered vocals. Cold neon, menace, rage, survival. Analog synths only, no acoustic instruments, no orchestral elements, no growling.
