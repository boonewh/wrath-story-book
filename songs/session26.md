# Session 26 — Even Demons Can Dream

**POV:** Caleth  
**Title:** Even Demons Can Dream

For **Session 26**, I would give Caleth a more energetic, battle-driven 80s synthwave song than No Shadow, while keeping that darker, cinematic atmosphere.

This session has three powerful themes: trust, redemption, and the determination to confront Xanthir Vang. Caleth questions Aravashnial's behavior, fights through a demon-infested ruin, discovers that a succubus might genuinely be changing, and ultimately declares that Arueshalae is under his protection.

The contrast is what makes this interesting: Caleth is suspicious of someone he knows, yet eventually stands up for someone he has every reason to distrust.

## Suno Prompt

Epic cinematic 1980s synthwave, dark synth-rock, male lead vocals, determined and emotional, 115–120 BPM, minor key. Driving analog synth bass, pulsing arpeggiators, gated reverb drums, vintage synthesizers, soaring 80s electric guitar solos, atmospheric pads, dramatic tom fills. Mysterious opening with distant silver bells and ominous synth textures. Verses have rhythmic, urgent storytelling vocals. Huge melodic anthemic choruses with layered male backing vocals, powerful emotional hooks and retro arena-rock energy. Fast-paced instrumental combat section with aggressive synth bass and electric guitar. Quiet atmospheric breakdown with haunting pads and intimate vocals during the redemption story. Gradual cinematic build into an explosive final chorus filled with hope, defiance and determination. Outro shifts from triumphant to ominous, ending with a distant silver bell. Dark fantasy adventure meets classic 1980s synthwave soundtrack. Strong melody, clear expressive vocals, dramatic dynamics, polished production.
