🗝️ "Who Has Business Inside" — Dark Cinematic Trap-Rap Companion Piece

The **Session 14** song for [chapters/14-who-has-business-inside.md](../chapters/14-who-has-business-inside.md) — Thane's second rap, and the deliberate **companion and proof** to his character anthem [songs/character-thane.md](character-thane.md) ("Read the Door"), the way session 13's "One Word" stood as companion to Nageru's anthem. This is not an inversion of the creed — it's the creed *working*, twice, in one night, on two opposite verdicts. The through-line a listener needs: **a doorkeeper doesn't ask whether the stranger at the threshold is wicked — he asks whether they have business inside. Tonight he asks it twice. Once the door stays shut. Once it doesn't.**

Verse 1 is the first door: the suspicion he's carried for chapters confirmed — a spy caught, a credential proven false, and no joy in being right, just a cold room. The double-time verse is the second door: mid-fight, a woman who radiates evil by every honest instinct, and the doorkeeper's read overriding the reflex to finish her — pommels instead of points, patience instead of certainty. Verse 3 is the seam between them: same creed, same hands, two different verdicts, and the trust of a cousin who doesn't need the reasoning spelled out. ## Troubleshooting

1. **If it starts to sound triumphant or celebratory (wrong emotional register):** This is not a victory lap — it's a man doing an unpleasant job twice in one night and getting no satisfaction from either outcome. Reinforce with "cold, deliberate, procedural delivery, no triumph, no swagger" and pull any horn stabs or major-key lifts out of the arrangement.

2. **If the double-time verse drifts into a different genre or loses the words:** Same fix as "Read the Door" — it needs to stay dark trap-rap, just faster and denser, not a hype-rap or drill beat swap. Add "double-time flow within the same brooding trap register, NOT a genre or tempo-class change, words still fully intelligible at speed."

3. **If the Hook/Bridge pull-back reads as a full quiet breakdown instead of a held tension:** The point is restraint, not silence. Tag "strings thin, hats sparse, but the sub and piano hold through — tension held, not released" so it doesn't turn into a ballad interlude.

4. **If it starts sounding like a rehash of "Read the Door" instead of its companion:** The creed language should recur (listen/look/touch, "who has business," pommel/point) but the content is new — two verdicts in one night, the confirmed lie and the spared stranger, and the cousin's trust. If it leans too hard on lines that could be swapped in from the first song, tighten toward this chapter's specific two-door structure.

5. **Keep it distinct from "One Word" (session 13).** That song is Nageru's control shattering into one loud, isolated word. This one is Thane's control *holding* — quiet, procedural, and never once raising to a scream. No gang-shout breakdown here; the power is in the level, unbroken delivery.
