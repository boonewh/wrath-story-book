# The Counting Verse (Nageru's Song)
A monk's breath-verse this time — the kind taught to novices before they're old enough to understand it, echoing the same "there is always another step" thread that ran through Session 19, but turned toward Irori's road rather than a dwarf's tunnel

## Lyrics

```
[Verse 1]
One breath in, do not hold it
Home is not a place you keep
The road was never behind you
It was under you, asleep

[Verse 2]
Empty the hands that reach for welcome
Empty the ear that waits for a name
A vessel that is already full
Cannot be filled again the same

[Chorus]
Walk until the walking is the answer
Bow to no door that says arrive
There is always one truth further
That's how you know you're still alive

[Verse 3]
They will call you marked, let them
They will call you home, let that be true
The mountain does not stop climbing
Just because the summit sees you

[Chorus]
Walk until the walking is the answer
Bow to no door that says arrive
There is always one truth further
That's how you know you're still alive

[Bridge]
One breath out, do not chase it
The self you were is not the debt you owe
Every road that ends in knowing
Only opens onto one you don't yet know

[Chorus]
Walk until the walking is the answer
Bow to no door that says arrive
There is always one truth further
That's how you know you're still alive
```

## Style Prompt

```
low male chant, deep resonant unison monks, heavy stone reverb, 
sub-bass drone, slow deliberate tempo, weighty and grounded, 
low frankincense-thick atmosphere, taiko-style deep drum hits, 
no flute, no strings, no airy pads, dark sacred, monastery crypt acoustics
```
