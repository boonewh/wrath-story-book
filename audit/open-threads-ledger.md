# Open-Threads Ledger — Chapters 1–23

*Compiled 2026-10-02 from a straight read of all 25 chapter files (1–23, plus 8.5 and 18.5), checked against `CLAUDE.md`, `memory/`, `lore/` and `characters/`. Nothing in `chapters/` or the canon files was edited. Raw per-chapter notes are in `audit/_reading-notes.md`.*

**How to read this.** `CLAUDE.md` already keeps a long, well-maintained "do not close" list. This ledger is mainly about what that list *doesn't* have: things the prose planted that no canon file tracks, plus a check of the tracked list against what the chapters actually say. Every entry cites the chapter and line where it was planted and where it was last touched.

**None of this is a recommendation to resolve anything.** Several of these may be deliberately dormant. The point is to make sure they're dormant on purpose.

## Part 1 — Planted in the prose, tracked nowhere

Ranked by how much story is likely sitting in them.

### 1. Terendelev: "one of yours coming"
- **Planted:** Ch 2, L762. *"There is one of yours coming who will need you to be the kind one. Be the kind one, Spireborn. Even when the others choose the hammer."*
- **Last touched:** Ch 20, L907–919. Caleth realizes the *second* half was about him: "She wasn't warning me about them." Then at L1165 he wonders "exactly how much she had known."
- **Untracked part:** `memory/caleth-chose-the-hammer.md` only reinterprets the hammer clause. **Who is "one of yours coming"?** Nobody has asked. After Ch 23, "one of yours" lands very differently, because Caleth now has an uncle, a family line and a Spiral his father was afraid of. Other candidates: a child, a Riftwarden, or the Spireborn. Also unexamined in the same dream: "Carry it. Even when it is heavy" and "There is a great deal of dark in front of you. I am sorry."

### 2. The book chain (Ch 5 → 8.5 → 22)
Three links that no canon file connects:
- **Ch 5, L164–168.** The Blackwing librarian says Xanthir Vang "took what he wanted from us and he left." "What did he take?" / "We do not know." Aravashnial stays behind "to determine what was taken." No accounting is ever reported.
- **Ch 8.5, L119–133.** Aravashnial lists what the Blackwing lost (weapons, "a chest of rare artifacts that had never surfaced", crusade records), then stops, looks "genuinely shaken" and says: "There was one other book. … Never mind."
- **Ch 22, L1148–1204.** Vang: "I have another book in my collection bearing precisely the same mark… I've had it for years. I took it from another elf. Another Riftwarden… I killed him."
- **Question for the GM:** is the 8.5 book the Ch 22 book (Aelariel's, once held at the Blackwing)? Or is what Vang took from the library something else, still unaccounted for? Ch 22 says he took it *from the Riftwarden*, which doesn't quite match "from us" in Ch 5.

### 3. The Blackwing's missing chest and Aravashnial's sealed chest
- **Ch 8.5, L119.** Among the Blackwing's losses: "a chest of rare artifacts that had never surfaced."
- **Ch 20, L1171–1201.** A banded, sealed chest from an unknown sender reaches Aravashnial, who "went entirely still" and didn't open it. Still unopened at **Ch 23, L23**.
- The sealed chest is tracked. The possible connection to the 8.5 chest isn't. Flag only; don't assume it's the same chest.

### 4. Terendelev's body
- **Planted:** Ch 6, L332. A refugee rumor says the demons dragged "the silver wyrm's corpse off into the Worldwound for some foul purpose." The square is swept bare.
- **Last touched:** Never mentioned again in 17 chapters. Not on any list. (In the published adventure path this is a real later beat, so the GM may have plans.)

### 5. The two broken helms Korroc "should know"
- **Planted:** Ch 8, L54 and L378. "Good work. Old work. Familiar work." / "I feel like I should know more about this helmet."
- **Last touched:** Ch 8.5, L31–43. He restores both and gives them to the museum curators. "The familiarity went with him. It did not lessen."
- **Status:** Never resolved. **This is a different thread from the armor.** Ch 18 settled the *armor's* familiarity as smith-lore (Armor of the Pious). The helms came first and are still unexplained.

### 6. The pulsing wardstone shards
- **Planted:** Ch 8, L372. Korroc gathers the shards: "they still *pulsed*… He did not know yet what they were for. He knew enough to keep them."
- **Last touched:** Never again.

### 7. The other eight signatories
- **Planted:** Ch 11, L571–578. Durgrim Ironfist, Kazra Deepdelver, Harbek Granitehand, Orsik Hammerfall, Vondal Emberforge, Rurik Blackmantle, Gimra Coppervein, Tordek Runecarver.
- **Last touched:** Never mentioned again.
- **Why it matters now:** Ch 19 establishes a company of **forty** dwarves, eight "permitted" to come home (Staunton among them) and thirty-two captured. Were any signatories in that company, among the survivors, or among the escapees? Dagna arrived in Ch 23 and would know who's still alive.

### 8. The live antimagic cell
- **Planted:** Ch 17, L529–549. In the interrogation block, the eastern cell (Arueshalae's) is dead and open. The **western cell's sigils still glow red and its door is shut.** Caleth stops "four paces short like a man walking into a rope", Radiance dims, and he keeps the width of the room between himself and that door.
- **Tracked:** only Caleth's *fear* of it (`caleth.md` L210, "do not explain").
- **Untracked:** **the cell itself.** It's still warded and nobody has opened it. Is something inside? It's also the one room below Drezen that Ch 18's room-to-room sweep never mentions.

### 9. Caleth's foster parents and the Spiral
- **Ch 19, L881–893.** Over the heavy letter from his foster parents, Thane sees on Caleth's face "something Thane had no name for at all and had seen exactly once before, in a room with a false goddess in it."
- **Ch 20** is Caleth's POV and never mentions that letter. The contents are in `caleth.md` L188 (warm news, Klarah settling), and nothing there explains a false-goddess look.
- **Ch 23, L485.** The Spiral is something "his foster parents had named in careful tones and never explained."
- **Open:** what the foster parents know about the Spiral and about Talia and Aelariel. The Ch 19 reaction plant is dangling.

### 10. Irabeth's sword
- **Planted:** Ch 7, L626–645. Othirubo's letter says the longsword "once belonged to your old 'friend' Irabeth. She sold it to Kandro Nyserian… I've no idea why." Thane calls it "a small sad mystery about a friend" and says "Irabeth might want to know it's above ground again." Caleth carries the case out.
- **Last touched:** Never again. Was it returned? Why did she sell it?

### 11. Horgus's bag and Horgus's loss
- **Planted:** Ch 8.5, L171–177. Horgus goes deeper into his cellar alone and comes back with "a small bag. He held it close and he did not speak of it… a closed door." He also says: "I understand better than most what it means to lose everything to demons."
- **Last touched:** Never again. Horgus has a backstory hook and an object, and neither has been touched.

### 12. Nageru's prayer beads
- **Planted:** Ch 8.5, L193. Carved "with a craft and a care that did not belong to this century… clearly long-loved by someone." Horgus gives them up with a "kind lie."
- **Last touched:** Nageru wears them through Ch 9 (L6, L297). Whose they were is never asked. It may pair with #11.

### 13. Klarah's other name
- **Planted:** Ch 4, L678–686. Nageru says she "will need another [name]. A name we use for her. A small name, kind. A name a parent uses… It will come."
- **Last touched:** Klarah's letter in Ch 19 (now with Caleth's foster parents). No pet name was ever given.

### 14. The bulging wall under the fane
- **Planted:** Ch 21, L1115. The hidden room under Irori's statue has fresh blood runes over "the whole southern wall of it — a wall that bulged strangely outward, as though something on the far side were pressing on the stone." The restored temple's light stops at its threshold.
- **Tracked:** `fane-of-irori.md` tracks only "whether the restoration reached it." The pressing wall isn't tracked.

### 15. Enemies sent home, not killed
- **Ch 19, L1395.** The derakni is banished "entirely intact, with the faces of four crusaders in its head."
- **Ch 20, L181.** The bebilith "will wake up in the Abyss in about a year with a very poor opinion of dwarves."
- Both are returnable and neither is tracked.

### 16. Thane's secret about Irabeth
- **Ch 17, L311.** Staunton's journal has a page about Irabeth: "When Kenabres finally falls, I want her alive." Thane "read it once and did not read it again and did not repeat any of it aloud, then or ever."
- It's not in the Secrets Matrix. It belongs there, as Thane-only.

### 17. Smaller Book 1 debts (likely dormant, listed for completeness)
- **Ch 2, L232–238.** The invisible dwarf mage waiting by his own fire. "Why?" "No one had an answer." Caleth resolves to draw his face in his journal.
- **Ch 2, L674–704.** Chief Sull's message to the surface ("Nathholm stands") and "We will not forget Nathholm." / "See that you do not." Never delivered on the page, and Nathholm is never revisited.
- **Ch 3, L342, L798.** Someone fled the specimen room "warning someone." Unidentified.
- **Ch 3 / Ch 8, L456–460.** Thane's Eagle Watch contract: the two unnamed women and the report he was hired to write. Never closed.
- **Ch 4, L1072–1102.** The white-bearded Blackwing librarian whom Aravashnial silently recognized. `aravashniel-riftwarden.md` already flags "some may be Riftwardens." Note that Ch 5 contradicts Ch 4 on who the librarians were (see `canon-audit.md`).
- **Ch 5, L860–868.** Faxon, whom Minagho's letter makes commander of the Kenabres Templars. Never met.
- **Ch 7, L176–188.** "Let us inherit thine arms, Iomedae" read by **Nageru specifically**. Thane: "the why of *Nageru specifically* was a thread he couldn't see the far end of yet." Ch 21 (Son of Irori) could retroactively explain it, but nothing connects them.
- **Ch 17, L227 and L253.** "Torag — forged breastplate — markings removed" in Staunton's ledger. "Whose breastplate." This is a different object from the Armor of the Pious. Keep them separate, but it's a filed-off family mark somebody may come looking for.
- **Ch 22, L884.** The Woundworm's cache: "coin, and a scatter of small goods, and something long wrapped in oilcloth." Never identified.

### 18. Don't conflate: two "unknown tongues" in one clan
- **Ch 14, L308.** Jestak screams "a language, real words, none of them any tongue Thane knew." She's Grunhuld-Wintersun (Ch 20), so this is almost certainly just Hallit, which Thane doesn't speak.
- **Ch 20, L777–789.** Marhevok screams a language **nobody** knows ("not Hallit, not Common… not Abyssal"). This is the tracked mystery.
- A future draft could easily merge these two. One line in `the-drake-and-jerribeth.md` would prevent it.

- **Caleth's foster parents** know *something* about the Spiral (Ch 23 L485). That isn't a row either.
