## A1 — The Stonevein chronology doesn't close ★ the big one

The prose fixes four numbers, and they can't all be true together.

| Claim | Where |
|---|---|
| Drezen fell **75 years** ago | Ch 11 L457; Ch 12; Ch 18 ("seventy-five years ended in an afternoon"); Ch 19; Dagna's letter |
| The fathers **helped build** the citadel and lived there before the fall | Dagna, Ch 19 L739; Ch 12 L61; Ch 11 L604 |
| The fathers were captured **forty years** ago | Ch 4 L330; Ch 19 L37, 65, 327, 571, 601, 619, 769, 2005 |
| They had built it **thirty years** before their capture | Ch 19 L605 ("thirty years earlier he stood on this ground in the free air with a plan in his hand"); `lore/timeline.md` S19; `memory/the-fathers-survived.md` L14; `characters/thane.md` L51 |

40 + 30 = 70 years ago. That's **five years after** Drezen fell, while it was demon-held.

The crusade labels add two more conflicts:
- Every source says the fathers were lost in **the Fourth Crusade** (Ch 3, 8, 11 L600, 14 L122; `npcs.md` L219). Ch 8 L240 stamps the Fourth Crusade at **4692 AR**, about 21 years ago. That matches **Ch 3 L1076** ("for the first time in twenty-some years") and nothing else.
- The Ch 22 dossier (L125) puts the **Third** Crusade "over seventy years ago." That implies the book's own crusade calendar runs Third ≈ 70+ years ago and Fourth ≈ 40 years ago. That's coherent with the 40-year capture and incoherent with Ch 8's "4692."
- **Yaniel** (Ch 3 L1048) fell "at the gates of Drezen," lost "half a century," recovered "twenty years ago" — about 70 years. Ch 20 L1095 calls Radiance "a relic of the Fourth Crusade." It hits the same knot.

**The smallest fix set that makes it all consistent** (Will and the GM to pick; this is not a recommendation to edit):
1. Change "thirty years earlier" → "thirty-five years and more" (or just "decades earlier") in Ch 19 L605, timeline, memory and thane.md.
2. Ch 3 L1076 "twenty-some years" → "forty-some years."
3. Ch 8 L240: drop the "four thousand six hundred and ninety-two" stamp, or accept that this campaign's Fourth Crusade isn't the published 4692.
4. Ch 17 L67 has its own problems; see B5.

## A2 — The book's clock runs fast after the banner

From Ch 18 (the banner) to Ch 22, the elapsed time is about **5–7 weeks**: Ch 19 is "three weeks" and "twenty-one days" (L99, L621); Ch 20 is "the better part of a month" since Staunton's loot (L1283); Ch 21 is "the better part of a week" (L1209); Ch 22 is the same day.

These claims don't fit:
- **Ch 22 L47.** Aravashnial underlined "Ivory Sanctum" "four months ago." That testimony was 7 days after the banner (18.5 L303).
- **Ch 22 L61.** The study has been untidy "for something under four months."
- **Ch 22 L664, L674.** "kept it loaded for months" / "**spent four months** keeping a spell in a glaive."

The opposite slip happens the morning after the banner:
- **Ch 18 L805.** Caleth "had knelt in that wreck **four days ago**" (it was the day before).
- **Ch 18 L871.** "spent **four days** trying to hammer him."
- **Ch 18 L939.** "**Four days ago**, in a room with Staunton…", then L941 says "Not once, in **two days**."
- **Ch 18.5 L1979.** Aron's title was given "four days ago, in the courtyard." The same interlude shows it given at the council table (L99–117), weeks earlier.

And a slip early in the book:
- **Ch 8.5 L7** "streets they had held **for a year**" and **Ch 9 L40** "bones that had lain **a year**". It had been days to weeks.

`memory/campaign-elapsed-time.md` only polices "two years." It would catch none of these.

## B1 — Ch 3: the S. V. letter is missing ★

**Ch 3 L978–980:** `Caleth read it aloud, his voice careful:` is followed immediately by `Silence.`

The letter, the most important document in Book 1, never appears in the chapter, and **it has been missing since the initial commit** (`30345c9`). The full text exists in `lore/items.md` L197. Restoring it is a one-block insert.

The same chapter has two more gaps:
- **L836–844.** "Thane Stonevein slept… he woke ready." jumps straight to "He felt it as he stepped in. The room had a purpose." The descent and the opening of the last locked door are missing.
- **L692, L792.** Korroc and Caleth both mention "the ritual chamber we just passed." No ritual chamber appears before that point.
- **L866–918.** The lead Templar is "a man", then "the Templar leader… Smoke rose from the woman's chest", then "The other female Templar." And the narration names **Hosilla** (L918) before her name could be known; it comes from the letter's salutation.

## B4 — Ch 14: the dead don't reconcile

The roof dead are "tieflings, halflings… no defensive wounds" (L278–286; Nageru says "Succubus."). The barracks dead are "a dozen headless **human** corpses… a dozen **human** heads" (L300). Jestak says "All the men you saw dead. Mine." and "Tieflings killed my **halfling** crew" (L348–352). That's three accounts that don't match.

## B5 — Ch 17 L67: Thane's retelling to Joran

- The fathers helped dream the citadel "on paper twenty years before either of their sons was born — blueprints… a citadel meant to stand." That makes the blueprints **pre-construction**. Ch 11 says they were **reconstructed five years after the fall**.
- Same sentence: "and **one of them was never found at all**." That implies the other father's body *was* found. Every other source says neither body was recovered.
