**The Stonevein fathers' thread is CLOSED (Session 24 / Ch 24).** The GM's note on the session asked for it to be written *"appropriately heavy for the moment and the end of this whole thread."* Supersedes the open-status rule in [[the-fathers-survived]].

## What is now canon

- **Both fathers are dead.** Their graves are at **Sesker's Gully**, the abandoned village on the Khar-Zadûn graveyard and waystation, a day north of Drezen up the riverbed. See `lore/worldwound.md`.
- **Five graves**, apart from the old dwarven rows, on the far side of the hill above a stand of dead trees. Each is a flat fieldstone stood on end with a name **scratched in dwarven runes with a tool not meant for stone.** Two are **BORIN** and **THOREK**, side by side. **The other three names are unknown to Thane.** ⚠ *Do not name them.*
- **The specter's account** (Arlys Harnaste, laid to rest in Ch 24): the dwarves came *"with the group, years ago."* In its anger it attacked them; they beat it but could not release it. **One was injured and succumbed to his wounds. They buried him here** (Borin, who was already hurt leaving the tunnel). **They then left.** *"Some time later, two humans came back, and buried more here."*
- **Thorek was among the "more."** The chapter commits to it: he buried his brother, cut his name, **walked on alive with whoever was left**, died somewhere else, and **two humans carried his body back** to lie beside Borin. *("His father had not died here.")* **✔ Confirmed by Will, 2026-10-04 — and the GM has read Ch 24.**
- **Thorek's stone has a slipped R**, where the tool skidded and the cutter went back and fixed it. Thane, then Dagna, both stopped on it.
- **Dagna decided they stay** (Ch 24, the day after, Caleth folded her there): *"They should stay here… with all these dwarves who died fighting for Khar-Zadûn… Drezen was a place we lived. Khar-Zadûn was what they **dreamed**."* Thane wanted them brought home to the citadel, said ***"Aye,"*** and hated that he could see she was right.

## ⚠ What stays open — but these are unanswered questions, NOT hooks

The GM closed the thread. Do not turn any of these into a quest unless the table does.
- **Who the two humans were**, and how they knew where to bring him. The specter did not know.
- **Where, when and how Thorek died**, and how long after the escape.
- **Who "the group" was** that came with the dwarves, and who the **other three** graves hold.
- **Who cut which stone.** Thane believes Thorek cut Borin's; Dagna: *"a chisel, if it was Thorek."* Not established.
- **Whether Sosiel will speak with Thorek.** Thane asked (*"I want to honor my father… but I'd also like to talk with him"*). Sosiel: *"It's highly unusual."* **He did not refuse.** They talked once more; **nothing decided.** Don't resolve.

## How to apply

- **Write the fathers as dead from Ch 24 on.** The "do not kill them off-page" rule is retired. They died after the escape, free, not in irons.
- **Korroc owned the grief and still does.** Face wet into the beard at the grave; up the hill alone next morning; *"Aye, Ma."* The specter's *"And now the child comes before me"* was it recognizing **Borin in Borin's son.**
- **Thane's banked flame has changed shape a fourth time.** The answer came, and it is a door that does not open: *"there was nothing behind it but a dwarf."* What he carries now is **the two strangers** — *"he was going to carry the two strangers anyway, for the rest of his life… he would never get to put them down."* The question under the inquisitor's ones: ***"Did you know we were looking?"*** At the grave: ***"Hello, Da."***
- Thane's resting-place search with Horgus (a chapel; a crypt below the old keep that needs cleansing; a walled garden on the west side) is **moot after Dagna's ruling** but was never formally dropped.
- **The Staunton disagreement is untouched** and still unspoken. So is the possession.
