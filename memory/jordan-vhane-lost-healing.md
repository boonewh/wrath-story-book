At the end of Chapter 17, **Jordan Vhane can no longer cast healing spells.** Not one failed casting — the ability is gone.

**What happened:** Chorussina's last act was to put a **curved bone claw** (insectile, forearm-length, functioning like *vampiric touch*) into Jordan's back. He went to his knees, reached for his own healing with the practiced motion of a cleric who has done it ten thousand times, and **nothing came.** Korroc pulled the claw out — and felt it reach for *his* life through his gauntlet, and dropped it — then healed Jordan himself.

Jordan stared at his hands and said, quietly, ***"It failed."***

Korroc: *"Listen, it can be okay. Torag will take you in."* Jordan looked at him, **said nothing at all**, and went back to looking at his hands.

**FOUR LIVE CAUSES. THE GM HAS EXPLICITLY LEFT THIS OPEN:**
1. Chorussina's bone claw
2. The ritual she was performing
3. The destruction of the great crystal
4. **Droskar** — a god closing the door on a priest who had begun to look at another one

**Do not pick one. Do not have a character work it out. Do not resolve it in prose, on the website, or in a canon file.**

**Why it matters:** the timing is the cruelty. Jordan had *already* taken the step — left alone with his brother's corpse and a clear route out, he walked down the stair after four people who had promised to kill him, to tell them they had gone the wrong way, and led them through his own forge. **Nobody made him.** And then the hands stopped working. Thane's private read (kept to himself) is that a man reached for something better and was charged before he arrived.

**How to apply:**
- Korroc owns this thread. His offer of Torag is still open and the man it was made to has just been broken further.
- Jordan is **not in custody and not held by force.** He was told to come along if he wanted; the party moved on and left him sitting in the ritual chamber. Where he is next is a GM call.
- The claw is **still lying on the floor of that chamber.** Nobody picked it up.
