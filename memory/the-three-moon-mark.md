**New canon from Session 28.** The mark first appeared in the session notes as *"the same three-moon symbol, but slightly modified."* Will: put it in canon as a three-moon symbol, and let the prose decide the variant; *"if it needs to be tweaked, we'll do it later."*

## What is canon

- **Aravashnial's mark: three crescent moons set side by side, horns up**, *"like three boats drawn up on a beach."* It is on every page of his notes and the spines of his books, *"the way a smith's mark goes on everything a smith makes."* **His recovered spellbook (Ch 28) carries it unaltered.**
- **The variant: the same three, with the middle moon FULL**, *"like a coin set between two parings of a nail."* On a plain dark-leather book Caleth pulled off Vang's shelves. ⚠ **This variant was chosen by the prose (Ch 28), not the table.** Will may tweak it.
- **Caleth said nothing**, held it *"the way you would hold a bird that had flown into a window,"* with the stillness Thane saw at the infirmary bedside, and put it **inside his coat, left side**, not in the bag.
- **Thane saw both marks** and read the variant **the dwarven way**: Khar-Zadûn houses cut a second son's mark as his father's *"with a difference"*; *"that is Thorek's house, but not Thorek's hand."* **He does not know Riftwardens do any such thing**, does not know Aravashnial had a brother, and **did not ask.** It joins *"I have been told"* and the bedside face *"under the floorboard."*

## ⚠ OPEN — do not resolve without the table

- **Whose book it is.** The obvious candidate is **Aelariel's** (Caleth's father, Aravashnial's brother) — the Riftwarden's book Vang took when he killed him (Ch 22) — but **that is NOT established.** Do not write it as fact in any POV, and do not let a non-Caleth POV even suggest it beyond Thane's house-mark instinct.
- **What is in it**, and whether Caleth has read it.
- **Whether this, rather than the spellbook, is the book Aravashnial meant by *"He still has my book"*** (Ch 26).
- **What Caleth thinks** — Ch 28 was Thane's POV; Caleth's interior over the book is offstage.
