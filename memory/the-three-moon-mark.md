> # ✔✔ RESOLVED IN INTERLUDE 3 (Ch 30.5) — a reveal, not a conflict
>
> - **The three moons are the FAMILY CREST** of Aravashnial and Aelariel's house (Kyonin).
> - **The Ch 28 book is AELARIEL'S SPELLBOOK.** It began as **a blank spellbook Aravashnial had prepared**; Aelariel *"borrowed"* it without asking (*"That was the term he preferred"*), began using it, and **redid the cover with his own, more elaborate version of the crest** (*"I told him it was unnecessarily elaborate"*). That is why **Aravashnial's arcane mark is inside it.** The first pages are in Aravashnial's precise hand, then Aelariel's hurried, crowded one. *"My brother was a brilliant abjurer. And an appallingly untidy scholar."*
> - The interlude describes the difference as *"the arrangement slightly different, the lines more elaborate."* Ch 28 printed it as **the middle moon full.** Both stand; they are compatible.
> - **The staff's carved moons are the same crest, carved by their FATHER.** Thane used this book's cover as the pattern to rebuild them, and the old fragments matched it exactly.
> - **ARAVASHNIAL HAS THE BOOK NOW.** He asked to keep it (*"It was his"*) and Caleth said *"Keep it."*
> - Still open: **what is in it** beyond Aelariel's notes, and whether it says anything about the Spiral or the last mission.
>

 The mark first appeared in the session notes as *"the same three-moon symbol, but slightly modified."* Will: put it in canon as a three-moon symbol, and let the prose decide the variant; *"if it needs to be tweaked, we'll do it later."*

## What is canon

- **Aravashnial's mark: three crescent moons set side by side, horns up**, *"like three boats drawn up on a beach."* It is on every page of his notes and the spines of his books, *"the way a smith's mark goes on everything a smith makes."* **His recovered spellbook (Ch 28) carries it unaltered.**
- **The variant: the same three, with the middle moon FULL**, *"like a coin set between two parings of a nail."* On a plain dark-leather book Caleth pulled off Vang's shelves. ⚠ **This variant was chosen by the prose (Ch 28), not the table.** Will may tweak it.
- **Caleth said nothing**, held it *"the way you would hold a bird that had flown into a window,"* with the stillness Thane saw at the infirmary bedside, and put it **inside his coat, left side**, not in the bag.
- **Thane saw both marks** and read the variant **the dwarven way**: Khar-Zadûn houses cut a second son's mark as his father's *"with a difference"*; *"that is Thorek's house, but not Thorek's hand."* **He does not know Riftwardens do any such thing**, does not know Aravashnial had a brother, and **did not ask.** It joins *"I have been told"* and the bedside face *"under the floorboard."*
