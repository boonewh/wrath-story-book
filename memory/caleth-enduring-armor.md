**Caleth's armor is his mythic *enduring armor*, not the *mage armor* spell.** (Will, 2026-10-18, correcting the Ch 26 draft.)

**What it is (table mechanics):** *"You are protected by armor made of force. This armor grants you an armor bonus to AC equal to 3 + your tier… If this armor is dispelled or otherwise ended, you can reactivate it as a swift action."* Mage armor gives the same bonus, so it is useless to him now and he no longer casts it.

**In story terms:** he does not cast it and never needs to. It is **a close hard skin of force the wardstone left lying over him**, unseen, always on, and **it comes back on its own whenever something breaks it.** The line Will kept in Ch 26: *"the close hard skin of force the wardstone had left lying over him since Kenabres."* No numbers in prose.

**Why:** the old character file and README both said *"uses mage armor,"* and the Ch 26 draft repeated it. That was true before he was mythic and has not been since.

**How to apply:**
- **Never write "mage armor" for Caleth from Ch 26 onward.** Grep for `mage.armor` before handing over any chapter.
- **Earlier chapters are left alone.** Ch 2 and Ch 10 mention mage armor from earlier in the campaign. Published prose is minimum-touch; don't sweep them without asking.
- `characters/caleth.md` and `README.md` were corrected in the Session 26 canon pass.
