Established in **Ch 30**, mostly out of Arueshalae's mouth and Jerribeth's unfinished letter. This is the first time the campaign has had hard rules for the thing it has been chasing since Kenabres.

## The crystals

- **Thumb-joint sized, purple, with a light that moves slowly inside** like something at the bottom of a pond. Five were cut out of Aravashnial — one in each limb, **one deeper in the chest, bigger than the rest.**
- **⚠ Touching one with bare skin is not pain.** It put **hundreds of people's dying moments into Korroc's head at once** — the terror, the understanding, the going-out, all arriving in less than a second with nowhere to put it. Handled through steel it is bearable but not easy, and each one **pulls steadily at something in the holder that is not blood**, *"as though the stone had put a small quiet mouth against his life and begun to drink."*
- **⚠ SOME CRYSTALS STILL CONTAIN THE SOULS OF DEMON LORDS** — Arueshalae, flatly. **The chest one spoke to Thane** when he held it up in the tongs; Caleth knocked it out of the jaws. *"That thing was watching us." / "It was talking to me."* ⚠ **What it said is NOT established.**
- **How to destroy one: crush it.** Korroc broke all five with his warhammer on the laboratory floor — chip, crack, crack, burst — and each one let out **a thin coil of dark smoke that rose, thinned, and was gone.** Caleth: *"That was anticlimactic."*
- **⚠ WHAT BECOMES OF WHAT WAS INSIDE IS UNKNOWN AND STAYS UNKNOWN.** Destroyed, banished, or sent somewhere — the table left it open. Nageru knelt by the shards afterward and looked for a long time and did not say what he was looking for, and nobody asked.
- **Korroc has seen fakes** (Drezen's trophies) and **a great real one** (Chorussina's ritual below Drezen). Ch 30 is the first time he has seen one come out of a friend's arm.

## The elixir

- **The crystals are made into it.** Arueshalae: *"It is what they gave the demons in the cells, when I was in the cells."* ⚠ *That line passes without anyone stopping on it.*
- **⚠ FOR A MORTAL IT IS A WAY OF DYING.** *"It is not survivable. Not usually. Most of the ones they gave it to in the beginning died, and the ones who did not were not the same afterward. For a mortal it is worse."* Her reaction to hearing one had been poured into a man was **the first thing like fear Korroc had seen on her**: *"They gave him a potion? And he is still alive?"*
- **It is how you prepare a vessel.** Vang's purpose with Aravashnial: elixir first, **then put another being inside him.** See [[aravashnial-taken]] — **the transfer failed and nobody knows how.**
- **⚠⚠ MINAGHO'S PEOPLE HAVE PERFECTED THE RECIPE** (Jerribeth's letter to Hepzamirah): *"the last four demons who imbibed survived with ease… Minagho's serpentine scum and her insane scientist have finally perfected the recipe."* **It did not use to work. Now it does.** ⚠ **Two people, neither named. Do not identify them.**
- **Jerribeth had drunk it herself** — *"my own experiences."*
- **Hepzamirah** is in the Abyss **harvesting crystals for the elixirs**, which is why Vang held the Sanctum (Ch 27). **Destroying the Sanctum stopped one delivery point**, not the supply.

⚠ **Do not connect the green stones in the two anti-magic collars to the Nahyndrian crystals.** Those are green; these are purple; nobody has drawn the line and the standing rule from Ch 22 holds.
