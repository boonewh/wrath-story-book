**Rule (Will, 2026-11-08):** Caleth is Spireborn, but he did not grow up at the Spire. **He trained at the academy in Kenabres, living with adopted parents** because his biological parents were killed.

**Why:** the Ch 29 draft had him "trained at the Spire" or reading "at the Spire" five times (his magic sight, his necromancy reading, the Blackwing marks, his spell technique, his habit of saying only what he knows). All five were wrong and were changed to the academy. The README also said "trained at the Mordant Spire" and was corrected.

**How to apply:**
- Anything Caleth *learned* (spellcraft, scholarship, reading magic, habits of mind) comes from **the academy in Kenabres** (Kenabres Academy in the Ch 23 journal), or his foster home.
- Grep every Caleth chapter for `Spire` before handoff. "Spireborn" for his blood and green eyes is fine, sparingly.
- **✔ Confirmed by the player and GM (Will, 2026-11-08): born in Kenabres, trained in Kenabres, has NEVER been to the Spire.** The old "brief time at the Spire" lines in `caleth.md` and `lore/factions.md` are removed.
- **Possible, not established:** his mother was the elf and his father the human, and they fled the Spire so the baby would not be taken. ⚠ Conflicts with Ch 23 (Aelariel, the father, is the Spireborn elf and Aravashnial's brother). Possibility only.
