**New canon from Interlude 3 (Ch 30.5).**

## Who
**Three representatives of the Riftwarden Council**, unnamed, no insignia, formal unremarkable clothing:
- **a tall, gray-haired human man**, narrow face, trimmed beard, dressed in dark blue, used to being obeyed (the one who speaks);
- **an elven woman** whose face gives nothing away;
- **a younger human woman**, perhaps in her thirties, with a leather folio.

## What happened
- They sat in on Irabeth's debriefing of Aravashnial. Afterward the gray-haired man: *"The field agent has been compromised"* — **remove him from Drezen to "Council supervision"** and **"facilities suited to such work"** for **further interrogation**; *"His memory must be examined."* To Caleth: *"You are not a Riftwarden."*
- **Irabeth refused**: *"Aravashnial is not leaving Drezen… That is my ruling. You may report it to your Council."*
- **Aravashnial expected to be taken** — *"When do I leave?"* — because it is the order's procedure, and he does not know that he is NOT compromised.
- **Two days later, Irabeth's terms:** the Council may take **direct oversight of its safehouses and sensitive locations in Drezen** and review them; **one representative may stay as Riftwarden advisor to the command**; Aravashnial **stays, keeps his standing in the command, and may not be cut out of Riftwarden operations in Drezen.** Any representative must work with him, or she will not accept the appointment. *"I will convey your terms to the Council."*
- **About two weeks in, a representative tried to remove Aravashnial's records from his study** and lost the argument: *"That man is absolutely insufferable."* Aravashnial: *"he seems to believe every piece of paper in this room belongs to the Council."*

## Rules that come with it
- **Riftwardens forbid family ties** for field agents and officers. **The Council never knew Aravashnial and Aelariel were brothers**; Aelariel's marriage was never official; **the Council erased Caleth's identity** as a Riftwarden's son and took the parents' home, possessions and records.
- ⚠ **The representatives in Drezen do NOT know who Caleth is.** That is now a live secret with real consequences — the Council has shown it will take people.
- **Aravashnial's rank is Field Agent** (Will, 2026-11-21). The old "elder" rank is retired.
