At the end of Chapter 17, **Joran Vhane can no longer cast healing spells.** Not one failed casting — the ability is gone.

**What happened:** Chorussina's last act was to put a **curved bone claw** (insectile, forearm-length, functioning like *vampiric touch*) into Joran's back. He went to his knees, reached for his own healing with the practiced motion of a cleric who has done it ten thousand times, and **nothing came.** Korroc pulled the claw out — and felt it reach for *his* life through his gauntlet, and dropped it — then healed Joran himself.

Joran stared at his hands and said, quietly, ***"It failed."***

Korroc: *"Listen, it can be okay. Torag will take you in."* Joran looked at him, **said nothing at all**, and went back to looking at his hands.

**FOUR LIVE CAUSES. THE GM HAS EXPLICITLY LEFT THIS OPEN:**
1. Chorussina's bone claw
2. The ritual she was performing
3. The destruction of the great crystal
4. **Droskar** — a god closing the door on a priest who had begun to look at another one

**Do not pick one. Do not have a character work it out. Do not resolve it in prose, on the website, or in a canon file.**

**Why it matters:** the timing is the cruelty. Joran had *already* taken the step — left alone with his brother's corpse and a clear route out, he walked down the stair after four people who had promised to kill him, to tell them they had gone the wrong way, and led them through his own forge. **Nobody made him.** And then the hands stopped working. Thane's private read (kept to himself) is that a man reached for something better and was charged before he arrived.

## ⚠ STATUS UPDATE (Ch 18) — STILL GONE, STILL UNDIAGNOSED

**Ch 18 did not restore his healing and did not explain it.** All four causes remain live. Nobody in the party has attempted a diagnosis on the page, and Aravashnial — who spent four days at a table with him — never raised it.

**Where he is now:** **in custody in Citadel Drezen**, and **working the Purity Forge under guard** with Korroc and Thane. He surrendered to Irabeth without resistance; Korroc pledged publicly to speak for him when the Queen comes.

**⚠ KORROC CHANGED TACTICS, AND THAT IS THE REAL DEVELOPMENT.** Having watched his own armor decide what it belonged to without being asked, he stopped arguing at the man entirely: *"I'm not going to tell you what you are. I've been at that four days and I've had a think about it and I was wrong to. I can't hammer a man into a shape."* He handed him a hammer and told him to do the work. **Joran took it and said nothing at all.**

**The one thing that lit him up:** talking about the forge. Four hours of it — technique, timings, failures — leaning forward, using his hands, irritated at interruptions. **He is not being redeemed by argument; he is being left alone next to an anvil.** Keep it that way until the table says otherwise.

**How to apply:**
- Korroc owns this thread. His offer of Torag is still open and he has stopped pressing it.
- The claw is **still lying on the floor of that chamber.** Nobody picked it up. *(Ch 18 never went back for it — the return trip was for Idrian Vosner, and nobody thought about the claw.)*
