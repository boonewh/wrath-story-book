**A small thread with a very cold center, and the party never got to touch it.**

## What happened

In the **first scrying** (Ch 22, afternoon), at the far end of the enormous cavern where Aravashnial and the four soldiers were held, Caleth saw **a slow pulse of mauve light** at the edge of what the spell would give him. He recognized it instantly.

**An Abyssal rift.** He had stood eleven feet from one in the Ahari Gorge in Ch 11 — the one Aravashnial named flatly and certainly, the one Caleth started walking toward saying *"We need to close this,"* the one a vrock came out of while he was still reading it. **It took Aravashnial three castings and everything he had to shut that one**, and it fought him the whole way down, and it finally snapped closed on a demon like a door on a hand.

He saw it again for a second time as the first scrying failed and the water tried to follow Aravashnial out of the room.

**When the party teleported in hours later, it was gone.** Caleth's eye went to it by reflex and found unbroken dark. After the fight he walked out past the torches into complete darkness and looked properly, the way you look for a faint star. **Nothing. Not a glow, not a bloom on the rock, not a seam.**

## ⚠ WHY THIS MATTERS AND WHY IT STAYS OPEN

**A rift does not go out.** It is not a fire or a lamp; it is a hole in the world, and holes do not close because the evening has gotten on. Caleth's three readings, none of which he can test:

1. **Something closed it** — which means there is a second person in this business who can do what Aravashnial does, and he would very much like to know who.
2. **Something came through and shut it behind itself**, the way you shut a door you are finished with.
3. **It was never in one place at all** — the possibility he could not follow to the end of, on no sleep, in a cave, with his hands shaking.

⚠ **THE GM HAS NOT SAID. DO NOT RESOLVE ANY OF THE THREE.**

## How to apply

- **Nobody investigated it.** Four injured soldiers on the floor, three exhausted friends, and a mile of broken stone in the dark between them and it. Caleth made the trade and wrote it into **the ledger of things he can do nothing about**, which is a running Ch 20–22 device of his.
- ⚠ **It is NOT Nahyndrian.** An earlier draft read the light as a Nahyndrian crystal (the Ch 18 fakes, Chorussina's great crystal). **Wrong — it is a rift, and the Ch 11 comparison is the right one.** Corrected by Will, 2026-09-20. **Nahyndrian crystal is purple; the rift is mauve.** Keep them distinct.
- **This is Riftwarden business by definition**, which sharpens what Aravashnial's loss costs — the order exists to watch where reality thins, and the order is currently one man in a cage.
- **Location:** the cavern is near the **Molten Scar**, four days from Drezen on the **Gray Road**. The party can go back. Nobody has suggested it.
