In Ch 18 the shadow demon **Eustoyriax** poured itself into Thane and wore him — used his laugh, went at Korroc high-low with his mother's and father's blades, and was only stopped when Nageru put him down with two pulled strikes and Korroc shattered the crystal holding his soul.

**He came back, said nothing at all, and wiped two clean blades.** That was his entire statement, and everyone accepted it. **Twenty-one days later nobody in the party has ever raised it.**

## The point: that silence is a thing he built, not a thing that happened to him

**He made himself unaskable, deliberately, in about four seconds, because the alternative was standing in front of three men and saying *it was in me and I could see out.*** He was in there the whole time. That is the part nobody knows.

## What Ch 19 did with it

The chapter is structured to walk him up to the door and let him fail to open it:

- **He identified Jesker Helton's possession before anyone else** — first off the body (the priest's *heels arguing with his knees*, a man being pushed from both sides at once) and then off pure recognition, which was not a deduction at all. All he said aloud was: *"Something's wrong with him. He doesn't look like he's in control of his own actions."*
- **Caleth did not ask him how he knew.** Thane noticed that, filed it, and said nothing about it either.
- Afterward he stood **three feet away** and watched Korroc kneel in front of a total stranger and offer him atonement — *"just because a demon has warped your mind doesn't mean you're beyond redemption"* — and understood that **nobody has ever said that to him, for the excellent reason that he arranged it that way.**
- He tried. The narration gives him *"Say something"* and then *"You could just say it,"* and **a man who can pick any lock ever made could not open that one.** He told himself *later*, knew exactly what kind of word that was (he had used it at the vampire-spawn cages in Ch 17 and did go back for those), **and let it stand.**

## How to apply

- ⚠ **DO NOT RESOLVE THIS OFF-SCREEN, and do not have another character gently draw it out of him in a quiet scene.** When it comes out it has to cost him something to say, and it should probably cost him in front of somebody rather than to them.
- **Korroc is the obvious confidant and is therefore the hardest one** — the hands in question tried to kill him.
- **Nageru is the one who actually struck him**, pulled both blows, and apologized while doing it. That is a door nobody has used.
- **Caleth already knows something and has chosen not to press.** He has his own unhealed version of this (*"knowing wasn't enough"* — Ch 15, also never spoken of by anyone). **Two men in this party are carrying an unspoken thing a demon did to them and neither knows about the other's.** That parallel is the strongest unused material in the campaign right now.
- Jesker Helton's atonement has been **offered and not yet performed**. Whenever it happens, Thane is likely to be in the room.
