> **✔✔ Ch 24 — HE IS HOME.** On the fourth morning after the graves, a lone figure was seen from the west wall walking toward Drezen and falling. Caleth folded the four of them out to it; **three construct spiders** the size of bull elephants came over the rise behind it (see below). The figure was **Aravashnial** — Caleth's cry of his name is the loudest thing Thane has ever heard out of him. The party destroyed all three; **Thane picked the collar off** (no keyhole; a seam and a catch; needles out one by one); Caleth folded everyone to the infirmary. **He said four words — *"Been walking. For days."* — and went to sleep.** Korroc's life-bond is on him; Sosiel and Korroc cleaned and tended him; Irabeth stood at the head of the cot and did not leave. **He is alive, asleep, and has told nobody anything.**
>
> **His state:** bare feet black with mud and cut; robe in rags stiff with old blood; face bruised and swollen down one side, cracked lips, a badly-knit cut over the eyebrow, sunburn; **a ring of small crusted wounds round the throat** from the collar's needles. He weighed almost nothing.
>
> **⚠ OPEN — do not resolve without the table:** **how he escaped** (or whether he was let go); **where he walked from**; **what the constructs were and who made them** (Thane's read: *"It's not here to kill him. It came to fetch him"*); **what was done to him**; and everything he knows about Vang. *Also unchanged:* the chest, the safe house, why he never told Caleth.
>
> **⚠ THE COLLAR:** black, close-fitting, faceted green stone at the throat, ring of needles inside, **no keyhole.** Caleth jerked his hand off it: ***"Anti-magic."*** *"The wearer cannot cast. At all… But anyone outside it can. It does nothing to the one who holds it… We'll keep that. For later."* **Caleth has it.** ⚠ Still do not connect the green stone to the Nahyndrian crystals.
>
> **⚠ Caleth's uncle is home and Caleth still has told no one.** He sat on the floor at the head of the cot all afternoon with the collar on his knee and his hand over his heart. **Thane saw his face and knows it is not a student's** — see [[caleth-parents-and-uncle]].

**The single biggest blow to the party's support structure since Kenabres.** Reported in Ch 21 by **Aron Kir**; the party was away at the western temple and saw none of it.

**What happened:** the day after the party left, **a six-legged flying dragon** attacked Citadel Drezen and kept going for the courtyard. To draw it off while the courtyard was evacuated, **Aravashnial took four soldiers up a tower and began summoning creatures.** It worked. The dragon turned on the tower, **the tower half-collapsed beneath them, and the dragon caught all five men in the air as they fell and carried them off.** His staff came back broken in two, laid on Irabeth's table with a helm, a sword and pieces of armor.

## ✔ WHAT SESSION 22 CLOSED

- **What the dragon was:** a **Woundworm** — *"a dragon mutated by abyssal energy,"* per Aravashnial's own collected notes. Red scale, **six legs**, and it **breathes acid**, which is the wrong breath for the color and is the whole tell that something was done to it. Its name was **Scorizscar**, and it was female.
- **The dragon is DEAD.** Nageru killed it with his fists, five strikes, at the end of a fight it had spent mauling Korroc and then him.
- **Who sent it: Jerribeth.** Established by her own line in the scrying vision — *"What did she bring me?"* — **"she" is the dragon.** ⚠ *(This was previously flagged do-not-assume. The GM has now confirmed it. It is no longer an inference.)*
- **All four soldiers survived** and were recovered from the cavern and sent home to Drezen. Two were fine within the week; the other two slower.
- **Aravashnial is ALIVE.** ⚠ **FOR LORE PURPOSES ONLY — see the warning below.**

## ⚠ WHAT IS STILL OPEN

- **Where he is.** The party has no location whatsoever. The cavern where he was first held is near the **Molten Scar**, four days from Drezen on the **Gray Road** — but he was teleported out of it mid-fight and is somewhere else entirely now.
- **Whether the party believes he is alive.** ⚠ **THIS IS THE IMPORTANT ONE.** They scryed him twice and saw him alive both times — but the second scrying was **severed** by the collar, violently, mid-torture. *They know he WAS alive minutes ago. They do not know he is alive.* **Do not write any character as confident of his survival.**
- **What the green-crystal collar does.** Black metal, faceted green crystal, **needles driven into his neck**, fitted by Vang specifically because a conjurer and a Riftwarden is *"an exceptionally inconvenient prisoner."* **Aravashnial recognized it and was visibly terrified** — the first genuine panic Caleth has ever seen on him. ⚠ **Do not explain it. Do not connect it to the Nahyndrian crystals** (those are purple and nobody has drawn the line).
- **The templar who took him.** Put a hand on his shoulder and teleported out with him. **Nobody got his face** — not Caleth, who watched from the ceiling, and not Thane, who does faces professionally. Caleth's scrying attempt on him **failed for exactly that reason.** Unidentified.
- **His unopened chest** (Ch 20) and the **Riftwarden safe house** he was building in Drezen. Still nobody has gone through either.
- ⚠ **Do not connect the Woundworm to the shape that crosses the moon**, seen by all four in Ch 21. The prose has never connected them and the table has asked that nobody does. *(The Ch 20 drake is likewise separate.)*

## Why it lands hardest on Caleth, and why that stays private

Caleth went completely still and **made one small sound** over the broken staff in Ch 21. That is all a non-Caleth POV may show. The reason is Caleth's secret: Aravashnial is **the Riftwarden elder of his parents' order**, recognized his lineage in the underdark, and **still has not told him what he knows about them.** **Only a Caleth POV may narrate any of this.** See [[aravashniel-riftwarden]].

**Ch 22 makes this worse rather than better.** The answer arrived without the man — in a folded page in his own hand, left in a stack in his study, never handed over. Caleth now has the name and cannot have the conversation.

Thane's investigation of the fathers ran on Aravashnial's research (Ch 19); he was the one who moved the eastern bridge repairs up the list. **That thread has lost its scholar.** See [[the-fathers-survived]].
