**The single biggest blow to the party's support structure since Kenabres.** Reported in Ch 21 by **Aron Kir**; the party was away at the western temple and saw none of it.

**What happened:** the day after the party left, **a six-legged flying dragon** attacked Citadel Drezen and kept going for the courtyard. To draw it off while the courtyard was evacuated, **Aravashnial took four soldiers up a tower and began summoning creatures.** It worked. The dragon turned on the tower, **the tower half-collapsed beneath them, and the dragon caught all five men in the air as they fell and carried them off.** Nobody has found them. **His staff came back broken in two**, laid on Irabeth's table with a helm, a sword and pieces of armor. The tower still stands half-collapsed.

## ⚠ OPEN, AND MUST STAY OPEN
- **Whether Aravashnial is alive.** Being carried off is not being killed. Don't kill him off-page, and don't rescue him off-page.
- **Where the dragon took him, what it is, and who sent it.**
- **Whether the four soldiers survived.**
- **Do not connect the dragon** to the shape that crosses the moon (seen by all four in Ch 21), to Jerribeth, or to the Ch 20 drake. Nobody in the prose has connected them, and the table has asked that nobody does.
- **What he left behind:** his **unopened chest** (Ch 20) and the **Riftwarden safe house** he was building in Drezen. Nobody has gone through either.

## Why it lands hardest on Caleth, and why that stays private
Caleth went completely still and **made one small sound** over the broken staff. That is all a non-Caleth POV may show. The reason is Caleth's secret: Aravashnial is **the Riftwarden elder of his parents' order**, recognized his lineage in the underdark, and **still has not told him what he knows about them.** That conversation may now never happen. **Only a Caleth POV may narrate any of this.** See [[aravashniel-riftwarden]].

Thane's investigation of the fathers ran on Aravashnial's research (Ch 19); he was the one who moved the eastern bridge repairs up the list. **That thread has lost its scholar.** See [[the-fathers-survived]].
