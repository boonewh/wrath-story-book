**The largest single revelation in Caleth's arc since Chapter 1, and the campaign's clearest enemy thread out of Book 2.**

## The note

Searching Aravashnial's study for anything on the dragon, Caleth found a **fair-copy dossier** on Xanthir Vang — eleven even lines, no speculation, the sort of thing written to be handed across a table. Blackfire Adept. Servant of Deskari. Leader of the Templars of the Ivory Labyrinth. Destroyed the Blackwing Library and somehow commanded the demons that did it. In the Third Crusade he was still a man of flesh and blood. Operates from the Ivory Sanctum, location unknown. Connected to the Nahyndrian elixirs. *VANG IS EXTREMELY DANGEROUS.*

And then, below a gap, **in a different ink**:

> *Caleth—*
>
> *He is the shadow that wore a man's shape*
> *but cast none of its own.*
>
> *He is the thing that killed your parents.*

**The two inks are the point.** He wrote the briefing, set it down, and came back on some other day to write the three lines. **Then never handed it over.** It was lying in the third stack from the left, four flights and a courtyard from where Caleth slept, possibly for weeks.

## ⚠ THE MYSTERY INSIDE THE NOTE — DO NOT EXPLAIN IT

The middle line is **the Lantern Seer's third vision, word for word** (see `characters/caleth.md` → The Dragon's Secret / the three visions; `characters/npcs.md` → The Lantern Seer).

**Caleth has never spoken those words to a living soul.** Not to Korroc, not to Thane, not to Nageru, not to Aravashnial, not in the underdark, not ever. Confirmed by Will, 2026-09-19.

**So how Aravashnial came to write them is unexplained, and must stay unexplained.** Caleth runs four or five possibilities in about two seconds and every one ends at the same locked room: *ask him.* He cannot ask him. **Do not resolve this without the table.**

## What this closes

- **Caleth's parents' killer now has a name.** Nineteen years of refusing to accept the word *lost* resolves into Xanthir Vang.
- **The Lantern Seer's third vision was never Staunton Vhane.** `characters/caleth.md` had carried *"almost certainly S. V."* since the May 2026 file pass, with a parenthetical after Ch 8 saying it was unconfirmed. **It was Vang all along.** Update anywhere that still reads S. V.

## What it opens

- **Vang has noticed Caleth.** In the second scrying, Vang says of the rescuers: *"They were rather impressive. Especially the young half-elf."* **Aravashnial's response was to stop breathing** — total, instant stillness — and Vang caught it: *"Interesting. Very interesting."* **The elf gave Caleth away by trying to protect him, and Caleth watched it happen and knew exactly what he was watching.**
- **Vang murdered a Riftwarden years ago, took his book, and it was Aravashnial's family.** *"He was stubborn. I suppose that runs in the family."* — and Aravashnial, who has never raised his voice in Caleth's hearing, threw himself at the bars screaming **"YOU MURDERED HIM!"**
- **✔ SUPERSEDED BY Ch 23:** Aravashnial's brother **Aelariel** was **Caleth's father**, and **Caleth now believes** the murdered Riftwarden was Aelariel. The old rules *"the relationship is NOT stated"* and *"do not conflate the two dead Riftwardens"* are **retired** for that one man. ⚠ **Still true (Will, 2026-09-27): the Riftwardens Vang killed at the Blackwing were NOT Caleth's parents** — multiple murders. See [[caleth-parents-and-uncle]].
- **Vang is a worm that walks** — robes with no footsteps, a wet overlapping sound underneath, and a body that comes apart into thousands of writhing worms. The party has now *seen* this. It is no longer a librarian's word.

## ⚠ Ch 24 — CALETH BELIEVES VANG IS WATCHING HIM THROUGH THE SPIRAL

A one-second waking vision at Sesker's Gully (old paper, something rotten, thousands of small wet movements, a voice at his ear: *"Interesting."* — the word Vang used in Ch 22 when Aravashnial gave Caleth away), then a dream in which **Vang has a shadow, and it is Caleth's**, and touches the center of the Spiral. Caleth told the party: *"Whatever took Aravashnial has been watching me"* and *"it seems as if Vang is finding me through it."* ⚠ **That is Caleth's belief, not established mechanism. Do not confirm it.** Vang's week-long ultimatum **ran out with nothing** (Ch 24) — then Aravashnial walked home. See [[aravashnial-taken]].

## Craft notes

- **Caleth's reaction is a finding, not a vow.** *"Xanthir Vang must die"* — said almost inaudibly in an empty study, and heard by Nageru, who was the only one there. **Caleth Aranor does not make vows in rooms**, and the narration says so twice. Keep that register.
- **The chapter ends on the name**, spoken to a room, as the reader's first encounter with it in this context. The GM asked specifically that **the voice in the dark never be named** and that Caleth's last line be the reveal. Honored in Ch 22.
- **Nageru was present for the study scene** and heard the muttered line. **He does not know what the note says.** Nobody but Caleth has read it.
- Caleth folded the page and put it in the **innermost coat pocket, against the true resurrection potion, over his heart** — not in the spellbook, because the spellbook holds the Stonevein blueprints and those are owed to other people.
