A **low, continuous, wordless whispering** began in the prison rows below Citadel Drezen in Chapter 17, immediately after the party destroyed two specters. It came from everywhere at once, had no direction, and was pitched under speech. Nobody could make out a word.

**Everyone in the party assumed it was Chorussina's ritual, because that fit.** Thane specifically notices himself relaxing once he has a name for it, and walks six rooms on that assumption.

**It was not the ritual.** It did not stop when:
- Chorussina died (Caleth's mythic shocking grasp)
- All three schir demons died
- The great purple crystal cracked, fell, and shattered
- The candle-ring went out and the circle was broken

Korroc's line at the end — ***"I thought that was the ritual"*** — is **the realization, not a question.** Per the GM: in play this was a recurring will save that the party kept passing because it is not a hard DC.

**What is unknown:** the source, the direction, what it wants, and whether it is connected to **Eustoyriax** (the shadow demon keeping the true Sword of Valor, somewhere deeper) or to something else entirely.

**How to apply:**
- **Do not explain the whisper.** Do not attribute it to Chorussina, the crystal, the forge, the salamanders, or Deskari's shrine in retrospect. It is an unresolved live thread — see [[factions-live-threads]] equivalents in `lore/factions.md`.
- It should keep running under scenes below Drezen until the table resolves it. Treat it as ambient dread that the reader has been told, explicitly, is *not* accounted for.
- The party's mistake is worth keeping in their heads: they took the first explanation that fit. Thane in particular knows he did it.
