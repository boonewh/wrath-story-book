## The legendary items (Ch 27)

- **Korroc's shield, Thane's punch dagger, Nageru's amulet.** Each had been taking in the wardstone's power from being carried close. **Nageru felt they could meet it and draw it out**, *"bring out, not put in,"* and guided **most of a day's meditation on the Purity Forge floor.** **Joran watched.**
- **Table mechanics (Will):** the mythic path ability **legendary item**, plus **upgradable**, meaning the item can be improved further by ritual. **Gained at earlier tier bumps; Session 27 was only when they did the ritual. No advancement.**
- **No visible change** (Will): *"The characters would know."* Korroc's POV renders it as **grain**: the item's and his own running the same way, *"took"* like a weld. **There is more in them for later sittings.**
- **Nageru's amulet is an amulet of mighty fists.** It is new to the page, not new to him (Will doesn't mention items that don't change the story). It is worn under his tunic on a cord.
- **Caleth did not take part**; he was copying the mounts working and carrying Irabeth's twenty to the bell. **Radiance is not one of these.** In Ch 27 (Will's request) the three talk it over: Radiance was *"already something"* before Caleth had it and chose him, so Nageru guesses *"perhaps with him it goes the other way."* ⚠ **That is the characters' guess, not a ruling.** Whether Caleth has, or will have, a legendary item is not established. *"We'll tell him after. He can be put out about it then."*

**How to apply:** never name the items' powers or numbers in prose. Show it in how a blow lands, or how the thing feels in the hand. When a future session has them "upgrade" an item, it is another sitting, and it is work.

## Fiendsplitter is in Drezen (Ch 27)

- **The table left it behind** because it had become **too big a distraction**: it would want Arueshalae every hour on the road. *(This was not in the session notes. Will added it after the POV call.)*
- **Korroc lent it to Irabeth** on the command-room table the morning they left: it talks in your head, in Dwarven, several of it; it knows demons coming, so pick it up if anything comes at the walls; *"don't hold it by the haft"*; ***"Don't let it think it's been left. It hasn't. I'm coming back for it."***
- **It was still sulking (since the tower, Ch 26) and said nothing**, not even at the handover.
- **Korroc keeps reaching over his shoulder for it.** Ch 27 ends on him in the Sanctum wishing he had brought it.

**How to apply:** Korroc fights with the **warhammer** until it comes back. **Do not write Fiendsplitter into a fight away from Drezen.** If Drezen is attacked while the party is away, Irabeth has it; whether she uses it is open. Its powers beyond talking and sensing demons are still not established.
