**Rule: about a year has passed in the campaign.** As of Chapter 21, the party has been together **roughly one year** since the fall of the Kenabres wardstone on Armasse. Settled by Will, 2026-09-13: *"We are good saying a year has passed."*

**Why:** the official timeline of the Fifth Crusade runs **about two years in total**, start to finish. The party cannot have spent two years together when the crusade itself is only about halfway along. Prose kept drifting to "two years" as a vague way to say "a long time," and Will had already corrected it more than once before it was written down. By Ch 21 it had spread to nine places across four chapters, a character file and a memory file.

## How to apply

- **Party time is "a year," "almost a year," "a year of walking into the dark," or unquantified** ("since Kenabres," "all this way"). **Never "two years."** Never anything longer.
- **Time only moves forward from here.** If the story needs to say how long, measure it against the last chapter that said so. Don't round up for effect.
- **When a line needs weight, don't reach for a bigger number.** "A year" carries it. So does the specific thing that happened in that time.
- **Before handing over any chapter, song or canon pass, grep it:**
  `grep -niE "(two|three|several) years" <file>`
  and check every hit against this rule.

## What this rule does NOT cover. Leave these alone.

- **Pre-campaign backstory.** Thane's Mendev camp drills and his Kenabres alley years, Nageru's upbringing at the Sunken Fist, the characters' ages. Those happened before Armasse.
- **Outside timelines.** *Maranse Delaskru disappeared about two years ago* (Ch 16) is when he vanished in the world, not how long the party has been anywhere. It is fine.
- **The Stonevein fathers digging "better than a year"** decades ago, the seventy-five years since Drezen fell, and similar historical spans.

*(Side note for a future pass, not part of this rule: Thane's own backstory is internally inconsistent. Ch 3 and `thane.md` give **two** years in the Kenabres alleys; Ch 11 says **three** years in the gutters. That's pre-campaign and was left untouched. Ask before reconciling it.)*
