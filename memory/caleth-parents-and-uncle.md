**The answer to the question Caleth's whole arc was built on, delivered in Session 23 — on paper, from a man who cannot be asked about it.**

## What is now canon

- **Father: AELARIEL** — a **Spireborn elf**, a **Riftwarden**, and **Aravashnial's brother.** No second name on the record.
- **Mother: TALIA ARANOR** — **human**, a **Riftwarden.** Caleth carries **her** family name, chosen on purpose over the objections of *"some among our people."*
- **Caleth Aranor was born in Kenabres**, recorded **14 Sarenith 4689 AR** (so he is about twenty-five). The **Seeker's Spiral was on him at birth.** *(Aelariel's letter as written by the GM said "the morning after"; Will corrected it — from birth. The chapter prints the corrected line.)*
- **Both parents were killed by Xanthir Vang** (Ch 22 note). They vanished into the Worldwound less than a month after his birth.
- **ARAVASHNIAL IS CALETH'S UNCLE.** ⚠ **Only Caleth knows.** He has told no one — not even when Dagna asked who found the escape tunnel (*"His name is Aravashnial… He is also missing"*).
- **Caleth now believes the Riftwarden Vang boasted of killing in Ch 22 — the elf whose book Vang keeps, the one Aravashnial screamed "YOU MURDERED HIM!" about — was Aelariel, his father.** Clinched for him by Talia's *"stubbornness… runs in the blood"* against Vang's *"I suppose that runs in the family."* *(In Ch 22 he assumed it was the elf's private grief. Vang's **Blackwing** victims are separate people and were NOT Caleth's parents — Will, 2026-09-27.)*

## Where it came from

1. **Aravashnial's locked journal** — the only thing Caleth ever saw him lock, opened by Thane in the study. Decades deep, and full of Caleth: birth, foster family, **Kenabres Academy** graduation, and **a childhood fever (~age 8) broken by medicine sent anonymously** (real incident, GM-confirmed; keep the details vague). Inside it, **Caleth's birth certificate, burned around every edge.** Also the elf's **untested theory of Vang**: a Third Crusade human who died and was raised by **a blessing from Deskari** for some act Aravashnial never learned.
2. **Two letters sewn into the cover of Aravashnial's field journal**, which Vang's quasit threw onto the north parapet torn apart. **Nageru** felt the seam. Full texts are in `chapters/23-children-are-not-equations.md`.
   - **Talia → Aravashnial**, announcing the pregnancy. Warm, teasing. *"Children are not equations." / "He informed me that this did not prevent them from having variables."* Postscript from Aelariel: *"Ignore at least half of what she has written. The other half is accurate."*
   - **Aelariel → "Brother"**, three days after the birth. *"His name is Caleth Aranor."* Then the Spiral, and ***"Brother, I am afraid… Come home, Aravashnial… Then I need my brother here."*** **Complete; unsigned.**

## ⚠ WHAT STAYS OPEN — do not resolve without the table

- **Why Aravashnial never told him.** The journal does not say.
- **Whether Aravashnial came home when his brother begged**, and whether he was there when they went.
- **What "the records" and "the stories" say about the Seeker's Spiral** — the thing his father was afraid of. **Do not invent them.**
- **What the parents' mission was, where they died, how Vang killed them.**
- **Who burned the certificate**, and how Aravashnial came by it.
- **How Aravashnial knew the Lantern Seer's third vision** (from Ch 22). Caleth hasn't found it in the journal "yet."

## How to apply

- **Secrets matrix:** Korroc, Thane, Nageru, Irabeth and everyone else know only that Caleth read two old letters on the parapet and went quiet. **Nageru found them and does not know what they say.** Non-Caleth POVs may show the reaction, never the content.
- **The refrain is *"children are not equations."*** Caleth recognized himself in his father — the man who runs the futures — and his mother's line is the counterweight. On the night of the letters, **he could not run the futures** for the first time he can remember. Use sparingly.
- **What he carries:** innermost coat pocket, over his heart — the resurrection potion, the Vang note, both letters. Opposite inner pocket — the journal, with the certificate inside.
- The GM's prop certificate spells the city *"Kenebras"* (an AI typo). **Canon is Kenabres.** Signatories on the prop: *Ilvessan Aeleth*, Keeper of Records; *Elandor Vhosst*, Magister of the Council.
- ⚠ **Flagged, unresolved:** `lore/factions.md` calls the Mordant Spire a half-elven community, but Aelariel is a Spireborn **elf**. Awaiting Will's ruling.
