**The succubus from the butterfly cell has finally appeared — in a dream, to Nageru, by name.** The dream was written by the GM in full (it is in `sessions/session25.md` and rendered in `chapters/25-the-bell-of-mercy.md`). She has still **never been met in waking life.**

## What is canon

- **She knows Nageru's name.** *"You are one of them. One of the ones who took Drezen… Nageru."* He has never met her or told her. He has spoken **her** name aloud twice — at her cell (Ch 17) and over Jerribeth's bounty at the fane (Ch 21).
- ***"I asked for someone who might listen before deciding what I am. And something answered."***
- **Hunted** through a ruined fortress **far to the west** by demons, cultists, and **an annis hag** who leads them — *"She does not want to kill me. She wants to take me back."*
- Hiding, barricaded, in **an old bell tower.** *"I cannot run again. I will not go back. I will not become that again."* / *"I do not want to be what I was."* / *"Please… Find me."*
- **The Bell of Mercy**, in the fortress's **southeast tower** — *"They fear it."* The dream opened on its note and showed the fortress **as it once was** (torches, armored figures, a small shrine beneath a tower).
- **Nageru carries the whole layout** and knows how to get there; he cannot put it on a map. **Joran** pointed to the place on the command-room map (unexplained); **at least a week west, past the Gray Road.**
- Her appearance: black hair, very pale skin, black horns curving back, dark wings, **a bow**, red cloth, dried blood along one arm, an arrow-torn wing. **She kept her distance and never touched him.** She glanced at his **prayer beads** (Will: the beads, not a holy symbol): *"A demon asking a holy man for help. I understand the absurdity."*
- **The party knows it is Arueshalae** and said so openly. **They are going.** Aravashnial named the giant an **annis hag** and recalled the **Sifkesh-sworn annis Staunton hired** to track her — the party's inference that it is the same one.

## The table's own argument (use it in character; it was said at the table)
- Andrew (Nageru): could something be acting on her — mythic or magical — changing what she is?
- Richard (Caleth): *"I've always thought that a demon is a demon."*
- Andrew: *"I know, that's why I was wondering."*

## ⚠ OPEN — do not resolve without the table
- **What sent the dream, and what "answered" her.** Nageru knows the dream was not his own and is privately afraid he knows what answered — it sits on top of *"Son of Irori."* **Do not confirm.**
- **How she knew his name.**
- **Whether she is what she says**, and **whether something has changed what she is.**
- **What the Bell of Mercy does** and why the hunters fear it. **The fortress has no name** — do not invent one.
- **"Take me back" — back to what.** (Aponavicius captured her and prepared an oubliette in the Rasping Rifts; nobody has drawn the line aloud.)
- **How Joran knew where it is.**

## How to apply
- **The secrets matrix:** the whole party heard the dream in detail. The inner experience (the frozen moonlight, the bell going through him) is Nageru's.
- **She can no longer teleport or go ethereal** (Interlude 2) — consistent with her running on foot and hiding.
- **Do not let her turn up in prose ahead of the table.** The ride west is a week or more.
