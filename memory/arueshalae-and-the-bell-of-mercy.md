> **⚠⚠⚠ Ch 26 — FOUND, AND IN DREZEN UNDER CALETH'S PROTECTION.**
> - **The fortress:** a ruined **fortress of Desna** through a petrified forest, a week west of the fane. **ONE tower** — the dream's two-tower layout and its cultists were **"just a dream"** (Will). Bell at the top, Arueshalae barricaded at the foot.
> - **The Bell of Mercy:** pure silver, **Desna's runes**, a hammer beside it. **Thane rang it**; the ringing **hallows the tower (only the tower)** for a limited time and **can only be done so many times.** **Dimension-locked and house-heavy — left in place.** The table wants it in Drezen someday.
> - **She is not hurt by the hallowing** — Caleth's proof; Korroc: *"She's not evil… Something's happened to her."*
> - **Her story** (heard by all four and Aravashnial; read aloud at the table, rendered in Ch 26): came to the Wound **4636 AR**; drained a **priestess of Desna**, slipped into the dying woman's dream, was **trapped in the Dimension of Dreams**; **Desna quickened her larval soul** — *"Even demons can dream"*; mythic potential and shame; hid it until **she saved a lost child**; stayed in the Wound to learn something for the crusade; learned of **templars feeding Nahyndrian crystals to demons**; **captured**; **Aponavicius tried to revert her and failed**; she **prayed and the wards fell**; fled **weeks before the party reached Drezen** to the fortress **her victim dreamed of**.
> - **⚠ She has been to the Ivory Sanctum and KNOWS WHERE IT IS.** **Location NOT established — do not state it.** That is what got Thane's attention.
> - **Why Nageru:** she **could not trust the dwarves — Staunton was evil and she could not be sure of their loyalties.** Learning they helped kill him shook her. Nageru: *"Desna may have had something to do with that."* *(Nageru's private talk; Caleth heard only that line.)*
> - **The hag is DEAD** (Thane). **No cultists**; the hunt was two drakes with incubus riders, a derakni, a hezrou, a bebilith, grimslakes (party doesn't know the name) and wisps.
> - **In Drezen:** Caleth: *"She is under my protection… If she is touched — they will deal with me."* Irabeth: *"I don't think you are the only one they'll have to deal with."* **She will help find the Ivory Sanctum.**
> - **⚠ STILL OPEN:** **what sent the dream and what "answered" her** (her story points at Desna; nobody has said so — Nageru's private fear in Ch 25 was something else) · **how she knew Nageru's name** · **"take me back" — to what** · the bell's full powers and how many ringings remain · whether the hag was Staunton's.

**The succubus from the butterfly cell has finally appeared — in a dream, to Nageru, by name.** The dream was written by the GM in full (it is in `sessions/session25.md` and rendered in `chapters/25-the-bell-of-mercy.md`). She has still **never been met in waking life.**

## What is canon

- **She knows Nageru's name.** *"You are one of them. One of the ones who took Drezen… Nageru."* He has never met her or told her. He has spoken **her** name aloud twice — at her cell (Ch 17) and over Jerribeth's bounty at the fane (Ch 21).
- ***"I asked for someone who might listen before deciding what I am. And something answered."***
- **Hunted** through a ruined fortress **far to the west** by demons, cultists, and **an annis hag** who leads them — *"She does not want to kill me. She wants to take me back."*
- Hiding, barricaded, in **an old bell tower.** *"I cannot run again. I will not go back. I will not become that again."* / *"I do not want to be what I was."* / *"Please… Find me."*
- **The Bell of Mercy**, in the fortress's **southeast tower** — *"They fear it."* The dream opened on its note and showed the fortress **as it once was** (torches, armored figures, a small shrine beneath a tower).
- **Nageru carries the whole layout** and knows how to get there; he cannot put it on a map. **Joran** pointed to the place on the command-room map (unexplained); **at least a week west, past the Gray Road.**
- Her appearance: black hair, very pale skin, black horns curving back, dark wings, **a bow**, red cloth, dried blood along one arm, an arrow-torn wing. **She kept her distance and never touched him.** She glanced at his **prayer beads** (Will: the beads, not a holy symbol): *"A demon asking a holy man for help. I understand the absurdity."*
- **The party knows it is Arueshalae** and said so openly. **They are going.** Aravashnial named the giant an **annis hag** and recalled the **Sifkesh-sworn annis Staunton hired** to track her — the party's inference that it is the same one.
