## The charge inverted, and he told nobody

For two years Caleth has carried Terendelev's cliff-dream line from Ch 2 — ***"There is one of yours coming who will need you to be the kind one. Be the kind one, Spireborn. Even when the others choose the hammer."*** — and read it as an instruction **about the others**: Korroc's theology, Thane's grudge, himself as the counterweight. He was rather pleased with himself for it.

**In Session 20 he was the one who chose the hammer.** He threatened a room (*"Touch my friend and be obliterated"*), won a duel three times over and enjoyed the winning, and ruled that a corpse deserved no rites.

⚠ **The death is NOT his fault and must not be written as his fault.** He offered a real bloodless out; **Marhevok broke his own word**, being demon-touched, chaotic evil, and already the murderer of three of his own brothers. *(This campaign argues for redemption often — Joran, Jesker. **This was deliberately not one of those.** No mourning him, no second-guessing the killing.)* **What Caleth actually missed is that he spent the whole hour on the man in the chair and never looked at the frightened people in the ring** — which is precisely what Korroc did instead.

**And Korroc was the kind one** — for four hours, on his knees in the mud, pitching tents and saying true things to frightened people, solving two-thirds of a problem Caleth had a written list about.

Caleth's verdict on himself: ***"I could not have done that."*** Not for lack of skill — he is better with words than Korroc has ever been — but because every person in that camp could tell within nine seconds that Caleth Aranor was **managing** them, and nobody ever thought that about Korroc.

⚠ **He has never spoken one word of the dream to anyone and this does not change that.** Do not have him say any of it aloud. Do not let another character name it.

## The one undo

He also carries **the true resurrection potion** now (philosopher's-stone oil out of Staunton Vhane's loot; **potency gone within a day of opening**, so it gets used in a hurry by whoever is holding it or not at all). Nobody discussed who would carry it; they all looked at him.

**He cannot run the future it belongs to.** Every road came back the same shape — *four men, a bad hour, a decision made by whoever could still stand up* — and he could not tell which of them it would be. And underneath, never spoken: **there are two people it could never have been spent on, and he does not know where their bodies are.**

He put it in the innermost pocket of his coat, against the folded Stonevein blueprints, over his heart.

⚠ **Do not spend it off-screen and do not let anyone build a plan around it.**
