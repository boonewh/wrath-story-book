**Rule (Will, 2026-10-24): when the session notes spell a name differently from the published Adventure Path, the AP spelling wins. Always.**

**Why:** the GM writes the notes fast, often by ear, and a misheard name gets quoted back as canon in later chapters. Will made it a standing rule after the Session 27 notes wrote **"Hepvamirah"** for Baphomet's daughter. The AP spelling is **Hepzamirah**.

**How to apply:**
- Before drafting, check every new proper noun in the notes against the AP (pathfinderwiki.com is the quickest check). Use the AP spelling in the chapter and every canon file.
- **If the lookup finds nothing, treat the notes' spelling as good.** Will's words. For example, **Jaruunicka** (the annis hag, Ch 26–27) turned up nothing either way and stands as written.
- Raw `sessions/*.md` notes are source records and are not edited. Record the correction in CLAUDE.md's name list instead.
- This does not override the table's own deliberate choices. Where the table has settled a name on purpose (for example **Beverach** vs. "Beverick"), the table's call stands.

**Session 27 cases:** **Hepzamirah** (notes: Hepvamirah). **the Green Gate** (Will's form for the place; the CRPG writes "Greengate"). **derakni** (notes: Derakani).
