**The GM is a contributor on the GitHub repo, not just the source of session notes.** She commits under the author name **Lori**. She has her own instructions file, [CLAUDE-GM.md](../CLAUDE-GM.md), which overrides `CLAUDE.md` when she is the one asking.

**Evidence in the history:** `f729f89` — *"add session 14 song"* (2026-08-30), which added `songs/session14.md`. **That commit is the whole of the evidence** — one song file. Do not infer a broader division of labor from it.

⚠ **She does the images now, and that produces NOTHING in this repo.** Image prompts are deprecated; see [[image-prompts-deprecated]]. **Do not expect an `images/sessionN.md` from her, do not treat one as missing, and do not suggest pulling to get it.**

## How to apply

⚠ **The song (step 11) is not automatically yours.** Before writing one:

1. **Check the directory.** `ls songs/` for the session number.
2. **Consider pulling** if the author has been working alongside her — her commits may be pushed but not yet in the local clone. **But do not manufacture a missing file out of that:** an absent `songs/sessionN.md` most likely means nobody has written one yet. Ask; do not assume it exists somewhere.
3. **Ask before duplicating.** If the author asks for "the song" for a session, it is worth one sentence confirming the GM has not already done it.

*(Step 12, the image prompt, is **deprecated** — don't write one at all unless asked. See [[image-prompts-deprecated]].)*

**This does not change who writes the chapters.** Chapters, canon passes and website updates are the author's (Will's) pipeline and are still done by the one agent per the Model Architecture Rule. The one that varies is the song.

**Do not edit files she authored to match house style without asking** — the same rule that applies to Will's own hand edits. See [[never-alter-user-edits]] for the principle.
