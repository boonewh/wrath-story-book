**Rule (Will, 2026-11-08, Session 29):** *"Typical demon death... they don't die on the material plane, they are banished back to their home plane and can't return for 100 years."*

**Why:** the session notes always say "banished back to its home plane" for demons, and the question came up over **Jerribeth** (a glabrezu, banished by Nageru in Ch 29). She is out of the story for a century, not dead.

**How to apply:**
- In prose, a demon comes apart (thin, black, *like ink in water*) and **goes home**. Never "dead", "corpse" or "killed for good" for a demon.
- Characters may know the rule: Caleth thinks it in Ch 29 (*"Sent home, and barred from coming back for a hundred years"*).
- **Daemons** (the thanadaemon, Ch 29) are not demons. The notes also said "banished" for it, and the prose treated it the same way. Ask before writing anything final about a daemon.
- **Not demons:** Vang (a worm that walks, **destroyed**), templars, minotaurs, the hag. Those die.
- Finality check before handoff: grep `dead|died|killed` near any demon's name.
