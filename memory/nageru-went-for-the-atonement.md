⚠ **READ THIS BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING THAT TOUCHES THE FANE OF IRORI.**

Nageru's player missed Session 20. In fiction, Nageru left the party at Vilareth Ford on the first morning and went back to Drezen. **His business was Jesker Helton's atonement. It was not the fane.**

**He has not begun looking for the fane, has not asked anyone about it, and has not mentioned it to a soul since Ch 19.** [[fane-of-irori]] is untouched. **Do not have him quietly make progress on it off-screen.**

## What he actually worked out, which nobody else did

Korroc had offered Jesker atonement in Delamere's cavern the previous afternoon and the rite had not been performed. Nageru:

> *"In an hour he will be in a bed in that fortress, being cared for by people who know exactly what he did to their shrine, and he will stop being a man who was offered something and start being a patient. A patient is a thing that waits. Nobody is unkind to him. Nobody refuses him. It simply moves down the list every morning, because there is always something bleeding, and a day becomes a week, and a week becomes the spring."*

And why it had to be him: *"Sosiel Vaenic can do it... But he does not know it was offered. Somebody has to be there who was in that room, and say what was said in it, and stand up when it is done."*

**Sosiel performed it over most of a morning, with Nageru witnessing throughout. Jesker is clean and says so.** *(Jesker, unprompted, at the farewell: "Your friend was there for all of it." Korroc: "He would be.")*

**The line worth keeping:** Caleth offered to stay for the rite and was refused — ***"It does not require an audience. It requires a witness. There is a difference, and one is enough."***

## Two consequences

1. **Caleth felt a small ignoble sting**, examined it honestly, and named it: Nageru had done a sum about a **man**, in a place Caleth had not thought to look, while Caleth was being efficient about wagons. **Nageru does not know that and must not be told.**
2. **⚠ NAGERU IS NOW SEVEN CHAPTERS WITHOUT A POV** and was not even present for the last one, and is still the only PC without a character song. **He is badly overdue.** The fane is still the natural spine of that chapter — and it is still true that *receiving a summons is not the same as answering it.* **Spend him when they actually go.**
