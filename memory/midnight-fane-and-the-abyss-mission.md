**Book 4 (The Midnight Isles) begins here, with Session 31 / Ch 31.**

## The Queen's mission
- **Queen Galfrey came to Drezen without ceremony**: **General Robard Kessin**, an unnamed **Riftwarden advisor**, the wizard brothers **Merrec and Davor Tolland**, and **a few dozen paladins.** *(Names invented for the prose with Will's leave; the table joked "Darrell and my other brother Darrell" and "General Bob" — the Tollands are the Darrells, and Korroc cannot tell them apart.)*
- **She asked about the demon.** Nageru: *"She has been touched by Desna."* She looked at Arueshalae, for a moment, as if at something she had been told did not exist.
- **The Lexicon of Paradox** — the book of forbidden rites that **opened the Worldwound** and destroyed **Sarkoris**. **Areelu Vorlesh** tore it in half and destroyed the half on how she did it. **Venture-Captain Varian Jeggare, Radovan and the half-elven paladin Oparal** recovered and restored it (there is a Pathfinder novel; the table hasn't read it and is fine with its facts being used). **The Scholars of Ozem** are studying it, not close.
- **The copper plates (two)**: a test. **A ritual to close a rift to the Abyss at scale** — one *"not meant to close."* Incomplete; the magic is in the metal. **Written in several languages, changing line to line — and Caleth knows every one of them.** (Why: not established.)
- **The power source is the four themselves** — *"No. You."* Close one rift and the Scholars may crack the Lexicon.
- **The target: the rift at the Midnight Fane**, where crystals come through from the Isles. Arueshalae pointed to it: **across the Worldwound, near its far western side; Aron: "two hundred and forty miles, at the least."**
- **Next day, bigger:** destroy the factory and end the deliveries; and **Nocticula** — break the Templars' courtship, *"or make our own"* agreement. Nageru: careful; *"bind her to a promise… it must be something that is in her own interest to keep."*
- **⚠⚠ THE RITUAL NEEDS SOMEONE ON THE FAR SIDE, IN THE ABYSS, HOLDING A PLATE — AND THE RIFT CLOSES BEHIND THEM.** They would need their own way home. **The Queen: since Nocticula is on that side, they can seek her out there.** ⚠ **Who goes through is NOT decided on the page.** Caleth: *"What?"*
- **Talismans of True Faith**, one per knight: help in the Abyss and help them avoid acts that would compromise their faith. Each became its wearer's holy symbol: **Korroc → Torag** (hammer and anvil), **Nageru → Irori**, **Thane → Alseta** (the two-headed mask), **Caleth → tucked under his robes unseen** (Shelyn, privately). **Arueshalae has none.**
- **The Queen goes as far as the Fane** — someone must be on this side to complete the ritual. *"I will guarantee my own safety."*

## The road
- **Arueshalae lost her ability to teleport**, but read **a teleport scroll** (the Queen's wizard had one ready) and took **Caleth and Merrec Tolland** to **fifty miles out** — on purpose, so a botched fold wouldn't land inside. **The Fane stands on a rise, visible from fifty miles.** **She has never teleported inside it: folds near the rift go wrong — "They exploded."** Caleth: *"Eww."*
- **Ten folds** to bring everyone: **Caleth eight**, the Tolland brothers one each. **The party: the four knights, Arueshalae, Aravashnial, the Queen and her whole retinue.** Arueshalae came, afraid: **she will go as far as this side of the rift, not through.** Cold camp, no fires.
- **Fiendsplitter would not stop about Arueshalae, so it went into Thane's portable hole** for the night.
- **Next morning the Queen in plain, unmarked soldier's armor**; her paladins and the five going in wear **Templar marks painted from Jerribeth's six pages.** **Caleth's summoned horses.** Arueshalae led them **around illusions** — the visible hill is a mirage; the real one lies south and west. **The Queen made camp a mile out**; the five went on foot.

## Inside the Midnight Fane (so far)
- **Camouflaged double doors at the base of the hill**, painted to look like the slope. Unlocked; they walked in *"as if we belong."* A long red-lit hall (A1).
- **A statue of Deskari** (many-legged, winged, locusts) at the top of the stair **booms in Abyssal**: *"Behold, the glory of the Locust Lord."* **Caleth answered**: *"Hail Lord Deskari, Locust Lord, may he rule eternal."* It went quiet. Korroc and Nageru understood none of it; Thane did.
- **A hill giant with a pickaxe** in a doorway. **An incubus** bowed: ***"The mistress of the Midnight Fane has been expecting you"*** — an audience. ⚠ **The mistress is NOT named. Do not name her.**
- **Balcony over a packing room (A3):** dozens of empty **lead-lined, padded chests**, complex machines of unknown purpose, **eight incubi** packing. Thane: *"They're packing the crystals."*
- **A5:** **a cambion** (red-skinned, horned) at an alchemy table making elixirs; **a gibrileth** guarding; and **⚠ a woman in Iomedae's armor with YANIEL'S FACE.** **Caleth: "Yaniel."** **END OF SESSION / CHAPTER — CLIFFHANGER.**
